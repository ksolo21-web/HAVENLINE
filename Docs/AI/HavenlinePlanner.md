## Critical path and milestone exit evidence
1. Production-quality opening environment: preserve the bright premium stylized
   snowbound direction, not the separate realistic agent-world project. Replace
   rejected flat terrain/repetitive simplistic trees/buildings with finished art.
   Validate early in actual Godot views and the close landscape gameplay camera.
2. Complete visible cast and movement: preserve exact approved source characters;
   finish distinct wolves and NPC5; check all four crew rigs and all action sets.
   Whole-cycle foot roll, inward knee/foot bending, skinning and transition review
   are mandatory, not just successful import or static skeleton checks.
3. End-to-end rendered opening: C1/C2 selectable lead; remaining original crew;
   one joystick and automatic proximity actions; unlimited logical carrying;
   furnace L2 = 18 wood AND 6 stone; gated 2.2-second rescue; north barricade =
   8 wood AND 3 stone; first 48-second/three-wolf wave; forest unlock. No invisible
   threats or frozen enemy logic presented as finished gameplay.
4. Full production and progression: worker jobs and chains, all ten connected
   biomes, navigation/travel and transport. Test economies, saves and transitions.
5. Complete presentation: weather, day/night, audio, effects, feedback and UI;
   collect stress-scene and representative content evidence, not only the opening.
6. Production identity/persistence: permanent signing/OAuth identity, real Google
   sign-in, live user-scoped cloud rules, save conflict and offline recovery checks.
7. Physical Android acceptance: install, launch, background/restore, folding,
   touch/safe areas and lifecycle. Sustain native internal >=3840x2160 at >=60 fps
   under representative long-session loads; record actual frame timing and thermals.
   Preserve aspect ratio. A 4K screenshot, FPS cap or software CI render is not proof.
8. Full-game independent critic and release: all original mandatory checks and
   required human approvals pass; weakest required score >9.0, target 10. Deliver
   reproducible source, verified signed APK, test evidence and recovery checkpoint.

Device profiling feasibility should be addressed EARLY, in parallel with art work,
not postponed until all content is built. Missing hardware cannot be solved by
changing the claimed performance target. Other independent work may continue.
