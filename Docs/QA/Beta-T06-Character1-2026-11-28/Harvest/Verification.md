# World One harvest yields

Scarcer deposits reward more each time they break. The current map census counted 34,794 sources across 1,024 populated chunks. Each pickup is worth 10 raw inventory units. [World census and opening check](World-census-and-opening.json)

| Resource | Sources on the map | Pickups per source | Inventory units |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Wood | 31,885 | 3 | 30 |
| Stone | 2,577 | 6 | 60 |
| Slate | 127 | 7 | 70 |
| Metal | 114 | 8 | 80 |
| Coal | 91 | 9 | 90 |

Regular wolves yield 20 meat, tundra stags yield 40, and alpha wolves yield 50. The player, survivor and snow truck get the same yield from the same source. Trees and mineral banks recover after five active seconds. A returning bank keeps its source identity and can pay again after a full harvest. Saved loose pickups keep their original amounts.
