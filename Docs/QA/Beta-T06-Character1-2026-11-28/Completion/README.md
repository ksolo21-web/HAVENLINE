# Havenline Beta T06

This candidate completes the Character 1 motion and interaction pass on the active beta foundation. Character 1 can lead with the automatic orbiting axe or join an existing paid survivor slot and use the approved two-handed tools. The original Character 1 model and both tool meshes retain their published bytes. [Source identity and preserved dependencies](Validation/Unchanged-verified-inputs.json)

The survivor's pick starts beside the shoulder, winds up, turns into the downward stroke, and returns to guard. Both hands track the shaft, and the visible cutting tip remains the point used by the bank's swept contact. The wider planting position and shorter pick fit were tuned for this companion's reach. The runtime pick fit is about 15% shorter than the previous companion fit. Four generic poses also use slightly wider arm positions to keep their cuffs out of the coat. [Independent review](Review/Independent.md)

The baseline for appearance is the owner-selected Character 1 design and the already selected beta GLB. The motion comparison uses the locked field footage, including reference A at 12.00–12.40 and reference B at 13.00–13.50. [Character reference and locked inputs](../Character-reference.json)

The changes are limited to three motion and stance files. The existing defense, economy, material quantities, conveyor layouts, storage and player orbit retain the audited beta behavior. Validation used the desktop renderer and disposable saves. Physical-device testing was waived. The current game has no player-facing dismantle entry point, so its T06 body pose was exercised in the action harness. [Prior material calibration and selected conveyor references](../Owner-Reference/README.md)

The independent review passed the user’s 9.9/10 beta target. The separate shipping policy requires 10.0 across C1, C2 and C5, and the reviewer recorded small timing and amplitude differences in the motion. Formal shipping approval stays open.
