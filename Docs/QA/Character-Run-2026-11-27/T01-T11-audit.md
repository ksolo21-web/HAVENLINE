The evidence does not support a single 9.5/10 grade for all eleven production tasks.** The latest native run passed 43 suites in 46 runs. A separate 16-process suite passed 217 checks through spending, interruption, service and reopening the earned Level 2 preview. **Balance and presentation details.** The field uses a 24-second encounter interval, an 18% alpha chance per formed wave and a cap of four live enemies. Harvest-source regrowth uses its own 5-second timer. The survivor can collect all six kinds when loose nearby; the current camp assignment actively harvests trees and nearby stone. Bought machines run automatically with input. The survivor takes goods to a machine and can carry the cooked order to the north counter. The footage proves these transfers without requiring a direct mechanical conveyor between the saw, drill and cooker. The actual mixer played 56 sound effects and three music states, including cues for damage and serving. Matching every sound or music cue to the ad’s soundtrack still lacks a source-bound listening verdict.
