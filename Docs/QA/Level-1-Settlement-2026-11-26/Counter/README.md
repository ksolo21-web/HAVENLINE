# Level 1 north counter

On the final paid expansion, the north wall docks the canonical golden customer counter from Alfred’s November 25 structure plates. Its 3.43-metre top spans the original 3.65-metre bay. The snowbound palisade ends at two timber caps and its existing wall collider continues under the counter. A deeper solid body keeps the characters clear of its boards. The worker stance is at world (2.8, 0, −5.46) and the first customer waits outside at (2.8, 0, −8.60). The two original gates remain the camp entrances. [Alfred’s donor and studies](https://github.com/ksolo21-web/HAVENLINE/tree/f2399aa25b969fe379df6387b9a4f66c4cc553af/HavenlineWIP/2026-11-25/fence-counter/docked-side-fit-v1) · [Asset and Git hashes](Source-assets.json)

Three skinned customers queue outside. The front customer reaches for the serving with one hand and pays with the other. A sale commits when the roast reaches the board with the customer still waiting and the living player still at the indoor stance. A player who steps away before contact keeps the roast. The receipt order advances once, the buyer takes the roast away and the queue advances. Reopening a saved serving restores its physical model next to Character 1’s right palm. The focused native test exercises departure spacing, duplicate sales, cancellation, attempts from outside, the death guard, both real customer rigs, receipt restoration and the physical payment.
