## Known service order and worker limits

Alfred’s first-arrival guard accepts a scoped game-Level-1 admission only after a paid camp, committed counter, eligible actor, valid ingress, committed matching receipt and sufficient typed bootstrap stock. An accepted arrival needs one durable identity and queue ticket. The service guard checks the approved front actor and matching queue ticket, requested material, exact prepared item, sufficient stock and committed receipt before returning a debit proposal. The recorded guard alone does not spawn a customer, set a recipe or price, receive money or grant a worker. At game level 2 or above the arrival guard returns legacy_unchanged. This fallback cannot advance the level. ## Binding used in this playable trial

The test plot costs two carried wood, paid one at a time. Paying unlocks the owl and lets Character 1 rescue the survivor. Once wood storage exists, the survivor chops a tree and brings a wood bundle back to the house. At the lakeshore, one carried meat yields one roast. The north-counter customer pays one bundle of banknotes for it. These prices and recipes are trial tuning. The preview button appears after the eight camp purchases, the survivor purchase and rescue, a saved wood delivery, an owl hit, a plank, a dressed block and a paid meal. Pressing it saves a Level 2 preview in the same snowfield. The production Level 2 destination and unlock still belong to T12/T32.

The source recording B has a known hash and had a soundtrack. A fresh inventory of Git and mounted files recovered native source frames and silent reconstructed clips. The full audio track is still unavailable for direct comparison.
