# Rescued forester and customers

The forester, the orange-parka customer and the blue-parka customer use three separate 1.50 m skinned models. Their surfaces, clothing and faces were reconstructed from eight authored views of each person. Each has sixteen skeletal joints, including the two feet and two palms. The moving sleeve interiors are reconstructed surfaces. The head material selects the calibrated artwork for the current viewing direction. Reference and native inspection frames are together in [the comparison sheet](reference-and-native-people.jpg).

The chopping motion comes from the recorded two-hand axe action in the Winter Welcome trial. Its arm endpoints and Meshy axe were scaled together to the compact forester. Sixteen measured vertices on the actual blade are swept against the pine's trunk mesh. Workspace files, reference commit and asset hashes are in [the provenance record](provenance.json).
