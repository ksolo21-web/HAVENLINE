# Survivor work and counter service

The locked recording A at 9.00 seconds shows the line on the public side of the fence, a low wooden counter, food at the handoff, banknotes on the inside and a floating food request near a waiting customer. Recording B at 8.00 and 10.00 seconds shows the smaller snowy settlement's single-file queue and a player serving across the fence. A at 42.00–43.00 seconds shows workers, food production and customers running concurrently. The reference manifest identifies the originals as the August 17 recordings. The north counter in the trial uses the canonical Havenline bench and register. The source banknote, sliced roast and the shopper's two palms are distinct objects. The survivor wears the selected Havenline forester and uses the same six physical resource bays as the player. The counter order icon is a native render of the model the customer actually receives. [Counter authority](https://github.com/ksolo21-web/HAVENLINE/blob/76645004d91dc6b05425f1ddc8a18017e701b664/Docs/QA/Level-1-Settlement-2026-11-26/Counter/Reference-authority.md) · [Forester turnaround](https://github.com/ksolo21-web/HAVENLINE/blob/01664a73ddf5087f17f7e159fa0b6b4ed71367b5/Docs/Design/RenderArchive/2026-11-23/selected/characters-survivors-npcs/survivor_male_01.png)

Each physical bundle represents ten inventory units in this trial. The requested 500-unit limit gives each worker 50 bundles of each of the six raw goods. Assignment priority, one roast per order and a queue of three are Level 1 design choices. Recording A's large crowd and its vehicle lane belong to a later settlement stage.

In the 360-pixel-wide copy of recording B at 8.00 seconds, the food stack reaches approximately y=330. The character’s head is near y=386 and the boot soles near y=429. That gives a combined height of roughly 2.3 times the standing character. The game uses 2.3 standing model heights for the six-material representative display limit. The source shows a food load. The requested six simultaneous balances of 500 are a separate gameplay stress test.
