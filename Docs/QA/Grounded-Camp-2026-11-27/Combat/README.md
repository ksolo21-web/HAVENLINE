# Owl protection and pack behavior

The paid owl now chooses the gate its owner approaches. When the owner stands within 1.85 m of the entrance, it can take off, strike a wolf outside, and recover while the hero’s axe stays holstered. Walking farther into camp recalls the owl. In the field it detects wolves up to 14 m away, catches up to a running player, and banks to intercept its target. Damage still requires an animated claw crossing the wolf’s mesh.

The control and candidate used isolated profiles, staged wood, real hearth and companion purchases and touchscreen input. The control held the owl on the south post and the east wolf at 3/3 HP. On the same route the candidate landed a hit from the safe side of the east gate with no tower and no spinning axe. The packaged review movie uses a second staged encounter with the player holding at the gate through impact. [Matched old and new frames](Gate-contact-review.jpg) · [Gate defense](Gate-defense.mp4)

Wolves anticipate a moving target’s position, retain pursuit as it skirts the pack, and find a route around the paid perimeter and the lake. When the player goes indoors they return to their territory, pause, and patrol. The unexpanded camp test previously recorded 884 consecutive pursuit ticks without forward movement. The revised wolf circled the fence, bit the player after 6.32 seconds, returned around the fence and resumed its patrol. Before the hearth purchase, a wolf previously took a shortcut through the inlet. The revised wolf reached the player by dry ground in 8.72 seconds. Both revised routes recorded zero stalled chase ticks. [Route and contact measurements](Combat-receipts.json)

The moving pack clip follows the real player camera at double pinch zoom. It contains two regular wolves and an alpha. At clip time 1.0 s the player takes a bite. Near 2.8 s the owl plants its talons on the alpha and the alpha flashes as the health bar drops. Over the combined default and pinch takes, the audio mixer started two owl dives, two impacts, six enemy attack cues and three player-hurt cues. The clips are silent visual captures at 15 sampled frames/s from Godot 4.7.2 on Linux with Mesa llvmpipe at 620 × 688.
