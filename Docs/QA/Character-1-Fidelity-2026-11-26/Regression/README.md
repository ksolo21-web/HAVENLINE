## Repaired QA findings

The first freeze produced two reproducible QA findings. The mineral migration test stored a coal position from an earlier layout, then compared it with a later metal position after the coal had moved. Its replacement checks the live position of every visible packet against every other packet and reconciles the measured counts with the ledger after each family arrives. The lifecycle fixture produced all expected gameplay results but terminated while five WAV streams and five playback objects were still on the audio thread. The fixture now drains playback and frees the world before exit. Three runs at elevated audio latency and the final suite replay shut down cleanly.
