# Independent Character 1 run review

**9.5/10 · PASS** for the revised empty and loaded run, direction changes and stop at the normal and pinch gameplay camera. The owner’s report reopened build 5’s earlier movement approval. I reproduced its long airborne split before reviewing the replacement. [Matched gameplay movie](../../Views/Character1-run-comparison.mp4) · [Previous issue](Build5-pose-hold.jpg)

## What changed on screen

With the same touch input, the old front boot and trailing arm often hung nearly still while the ground moved beneath them. The opposite leg then changed places abruptly. In the revised run the rear boot gathers, the forward glove moves through its swing, and the body turns into the new direction while the legs continue stepping. The change is visible with an empty rack and with six wood, two stone and one meat on the back. [Loaded turn side by side](Loaded-turn-comparison.jpg) · [Normal gameplay frame](Gameplay/0-1-096.jpg) · [Pinch gameplay frame](Gameplay/1-2-096.jpg)

I measured the time that the boots remained above 85% of their own maximum projected separation in each crossing-to-crossing half-stride. Across the four footage runs the median was about **0.175 s in build 5 and 0.100 s in the revision**. Character 1 still runs at 7.0 m/s. Same-foot cycles measure 0.567–0.575 s, matching the roughly 0.53–0.60 s supplemental movement sample. At normal zoom the revised character covers 3.64 projected body heights per second on the A-like diagonal and 4.52 laterally on the B-like route, against approximately 3.3 and 4.7 in the retained owner samples. ## Physical rig and limits

I tested 545 effective native poses at 120 Hz and released the joystick at eight additional phases, covering 592 post-release poses. Median planted-sole slip was 0.014 m/s left and 0.016 m/s right. The deepest tread dipped 2.32 mm below the snow plane in a release. The largest intersection exposed in the complete 545-pose camera sweep was 0.668 mm at a joined boot detail. Earlier prototypes visibly folded the calf through the boot and clipped the rear cuff into the coat. Neither survived in the accepted candidate. The foot loading surface was checked on both sides around the largest joint-rotation changes. [Foot loading](FieldRun/Foot-load-transition.jpg) · [Bilateral ground and flexion](FieldRun/FieldRun-side-ground.jpg) · [Release measurements](Field/release-floor.json) · [Actual contacts](Field/native-contact.json)

The original mitten shape, winter coat, geometry, skin, textures and 50-joint skeleton are preserved. Idle, Walk and the original in-place Run match their previously reviewed bone tracks exactly. The updated FieldRun has a fresh 120 Hz surface sweep, six camera angles and a review of every mapped part. The ledger’s 1,168 required rows pass against the reviewed GLB. The skill provider did not return its packaged release script. The included independent implementation checked the documented coverage and evidence requirements with the owner’s 9.5 threshold.
