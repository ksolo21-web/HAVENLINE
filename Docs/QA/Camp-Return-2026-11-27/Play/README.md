# Havenline Android playtest · Camp return

On your Android phone, tap **Download Android APK** on the private playtest page. Open the downloaded file and choose **Update** over the installed Havenline trial to carry your camp forward. [Private playtest page](https://havenline-level1-android-playtest.ksolo21.chatgpt.site)

| Action | Phone control |
| --- | --- |
| Run and turn | Drag on the play area. Even a short deliberate drag runs. Release to stop. |
| Gather or fight | Run outside camp and approach a resource or enemy. The tool attacks automatically. |
| Find home | Follow the compass and distance at the bottom of the screen. It leads to the first buy plot before you own camp, then to a gate. Gates open on approach. |
| Heal | Stay inside the paid fence. An injured player regains 3 HP each second up to 30. |
| Find an upgrade | Follow the construction objective to its marked floor tile. The tile shows its remaining price. Pause on it to pay. A partial payment survives closing and reopening. |
| Store goods | Use the storehouse entrance. The reinforcement and advanced construction tile sits separately in front of the house. Step off the finished tile and approach again to fund the next tier. |
| Work in camp | Take wood to the saw, stone to the drill, and meat to the cooker. Collect the cooked meal from the outfeed and take it to the north service counter. |
| Finish Level 1 | Earn all eight construction receipts, rescue the survivor, see the first wood delivery and owl hit, complete a cycle on both processors, and sell a cooked meal. Tap the Level 2 preview when it appears. |
| Zoom | Pinch the play area. The gameplay camera keeps its angle. |
