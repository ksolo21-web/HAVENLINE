# Connected camp storehouse

The storehouse gains its snow-covered blue roof, wooden crate crest and rear louver when the camp buys a connection to the house. The loading doors still slide and the gameplay camera lifts the connected roof at the entrance to show the inventory. The existing starter, reinforced and advanced shells remain available before automation.

Wood and stone dispatch through the left outlet. Planks and finished blocks use the adjoining left inlet. Prepared meals arrive at one rear mouth and leave the other for the counter. Uncooked meat uses its own lower port on the opposite utility wall. The front apron stays available for manual loading. Stock on the rails and shelves comes from the game ledger.

Both generated buildings were fitted to the existing working shell. The fitted asset uses the first generation’s crate crest, gable fascia and utility loading frame, plus the second generation’s rear louver and prepared-food loading frame. The blue tiling and seven connected snow banks come from the existing house. The irregular ridge was refitted after the first visual review showed repeated capsule-shaped snow on the generated roof. The shipping scene moves the cargo itself. The still cargo visible in the generation previews was removed. A native isolated replay bought both conveyor upgrades through the touch controls, opened all eight routes, and sold one real meal. Side dispatch left both pedestrian doors closed. A second run checked all three storehouse tiers, both front arrivals, six segregated bays, the generated roof’s cutaway and restoration of the original unconnected roof. The fitted opening clears 0.360 m over the central utility path and 0.373 m over the food path. The bound timber stack measures 0.298 m high and the cooked dish 0.269 m.
