# Level 1 HUD and the selected UI concepts

**The playable HUD is functional. The selected modern camp HUD is only partially implemented.** Both keep the camp visible, show a player portrait and resource thumbnails at the top, and keep the next objective short. The trial adds a live HP bar, an on-touch movement stick and return navigation. Its paid equipment and automation controls work in the scene. The source art’s 9.5/10 grade applies to the landscape concept image. Matching that visual direction in this build remains open. [Selected concept and gameplay side by side](Camp-HUD-comparison.jpg) · [Source gallery and qualifications](https://github.com/ksolo21-web/HAVENLINE/blob/9f74603283655313d16ec326558f19e546a8c99c/Docs/Design/UIConcepts/2026-11-26/modern-directions-01/README.md)

| Selected screen | Where it applies | What is visible in the trial |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camp HUD | Directly applies while playing. The image uses a single ivory material rail, upper-left objective with orange accent, a bottom navigation strip and an orange contextual Build action. | Dark individual count chips, a lower-left objective ticket, and separate Move, automation and display controls. The distinctive orange Build action, unified ivory rail and Camp/Storage/Survivors navigation are absent. Temperature and fuel are illustrated in the source. |
| Storage and upgrades | Separate full-screen management concept with a large house preview, item amounts, current and next tiers, and an upgrade action. | Physical storage, a stocked cutaway, nearby transfers and paid storage progression are playable. The illustrated management panel and tier-preview interaction are absent. |
| Survivor assignment | Separate full-screen profile concept with a portrait, slim status rows, task selection and a confirm action. | A purchased survivor takes jobs in the world. The illustrated assignment screen, loadout/trait tabs and manual task picker are absent. |

The three selected renders are complementary 1672 × 941 landscape concepts. Their illustrated values, assignments and costs are design examples. The source leaves device orientation open. Desktop Godot captured this trial at the Fold cover and inner display dimensions. Physical touch and visual acceptance on a Galaxy Z Fold7 remain open.
