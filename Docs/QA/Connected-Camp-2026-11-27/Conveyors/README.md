# Paid camp logistics

The first **Automate meal service** purchase builds the serial food connection. Finished roasts leave the cooker, enter the prepared-food shelf through the rear receiving port and stay there until a customer orders one. The outbound port sends the order to the guarded buffer on the staff side of the counter. A cashier or available worker completes the handoff. Money changes hands separately.

Buying **Connect storehouse supply** adds timber to the paid saw, stone to the paid drill, and the Level 1 raw-meat recipe to the paid cooker. Finished planks and dressed blocks return through the utility intake to separate, visible bays. With both upgrades purchased, a fresh roast can go straight to an outstanding order and surplus can go into storage. A fresh delivery and a stored delivery share a reserved entrance. The game considers roasts already carried by the player or survivor when determining outstanding demand.

Inside the camp, tap **Automate**, choose a line and approach its marked purchase pad with the listed materials. Only the selected line displays a paid plot. Existing machines keep working while the player moves elsewhere.

## Playtest results

The final native replay passed all 102 checks across 13 isolated launches with the connected warehouse installed. An earlier 97-check run preceded the added saw-and-quarry walking case. The complete run started from a genuine Build 9 checkpoint with three physical raw dispatches already in flight. It finished with three bundles of planks in timber storage, three dressed blocks in mineral storage, seven paid roast orders and an empty transit journal. A second v8 profile resumed a single partially delivered roast at 42 percent of its route and received one payment. [Final native replay](Final-native-conveyor-replay.json) · [Earlier individual checks and ownership changes](Conveyor-results.json)

A cook-only camp routed meat to the kitchen and connected a separately purchased saw without a second supply purchase. Moving a paid saw seven metres east and ten metres south while timber was already in flight still returned all six timber and mineral products. At the selling counter, a failed save left the serving and prior cash untouched. After a restart the cashier sold the original portion once and finished the first seven orders.

The tests also covered a failed save at a machine input, full outfeeds, storage and counter reservations, physical headway for two fresh roasts leaving the same cooker throat, credit conservation, and a legal warehouse move that disconnected a utility line. Stock stopped with its receipt and distance intact, survived an application restart, and arrived at the correct machine after the player returned the house to a connected position. Simulation resumes from its saved state when the app reopens.

One physical bundle consumes one raw receipt. The local hoppers and machine outfeeds hold four each, the prepared bay holds six, and its counter buffer holds two. The two finished utility bays hold 48 lots each. Quantities, prices and travel speed are Havenline tuning choices. If a finished-material bay fills, the machine holds its product and eventually stops consuming input.
