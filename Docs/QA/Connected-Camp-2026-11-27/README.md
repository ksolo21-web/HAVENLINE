## What to try on the phone

1. Drag to run and pinch to zoom. Both gates open on approach. Leave through the east gate, watch the weapon activate in the snow, then return to camp. The new compact Character 1 uses shorter strides at 6.25 m/s. Compare the working-foot contact at normal zoom and at 2× with a loaded pack. [Reference and gameplay side by side](Run/Gate-run-and-Character-1.mp4) · [Independent movement review](Rig-review/)
2. Pay for the storehouse, cooker, saw and drill as their plots become available. After buying the cooker, open **Automate**, choose **Automate meal service**, and approach the marked plot to contribute 100 wood and 60 stone. Cooked meals enter storage at the rear. A customer's order calls one to the staff buffer and a worker hands it across the counter. [Food line](Rails/serial-gameplay.mp4) · [Staff handoff](Counter/Staff-pickup-and-sale-2x.mp4)
3. Buy **Connect storehouse supply** for 160 wood and 120 stone. Wood feeds the saw, stone feeds the drill and raw meat feeds the cooker. The saw returns planks and the drill returns blocks to separate storage bays. With both upgrades a fresh roast can meet a waiting order directly. Watch goods cross the real openings on the side and rear. Walk up to the front doors to see the organized shelves. [Storehouse reference and game captures](Storehouse/) · [Expanded camp in motion](Rails/expanded-gameplay.mp4)
4. Select **Move**, select a paid building and choose **Place here**. Freight lines reroute where they fit. If a line loses its connection, the HUD identifies it and its cargo waits. Return the building to a connected location to resume that shipment. One legal test move kept six lanes open and paused the two workshop returns. [Moved saw](Conveyors/Independent-review/Relocated-saw.json) · [Conservation and recovery tests](Conveyors/)
5. Follow the rescued survivor. A normal game run recorded the rescue, the first wood harvested and deposited, and a later stone delivery to the paid drill. The worker can reach the saw and cooker while the belts are operating. [Independent worker trace](Conveyors/Independent-review/Survivor-routes-and-receipts.json) · [Loaded worker comparison](Workers/Loaded-timber-comparison.mp4)

## Source art, sound and review

The conveyor straight, turn and incline were generated from the supplied D01 render. The storehouse utility and prepared-food upgrades were separate generations from D03. Five textured Meshy jobs used 75 credits in total, including 30 for the storehouse. The fitted storehouse uses generated roof, rear louver and dock geometry with working sliding doors, cutaway and six inventory bays. The connected storehouse earned 9.5/10 after the reviewer checked timber, stone and a plated roast at their actual openings, both pairs of readable signs and the roof at gameplay zoom. The raw mesh downloads, inputs, tool IDs and hashes are retained.
