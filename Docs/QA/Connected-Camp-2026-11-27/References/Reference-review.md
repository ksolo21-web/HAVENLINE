The ads directly support a paid change from manual supply to visible local transport. I found no shot that follows one finished item back into the same warehouse that supplied its raw input. A storage-to-machine-to-storage return, the forester’s 500-unit limit, exact processor rates, and permission to serve every order are Havenline design decisions. Verify those through stock conservation, visible output, worker reach and payment in an actual playthrough. The supplied footage also leaves the actual skeleton, 3D root height and equipment scale in metres undetermined.

## Rigging notes shared with the team

Use the published stride data to check the order of extension, knee recovery and contact. The Whiteout coat often hides the knee. A clean frame on clear snow can establish sole contact and whether the body travels coherently above that foot. It cannot supply an exact internal knee angle. Align the playback phase to actual movement speed, check the blend into turns and stops, and measure foot drift in the running scene. Godot exposes root-motion deltas when that track is used. Epic’s published stride-warping method describes matching animated stride length to locomotion speed and adjusting the pelvis and feet. Both are implementation references. [Running measurements from Fukuchi and colleagues](https://doi.org/10.7717/peerj.3298) · [Godot animation and root motion](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/animation/animation_tree.html) · [Real-time stride warping](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/pose-warping-in-unreal-engine)

## Phone framing

On the Fold, compare the opening again after unfolding mid-run. Android’s continuity guidance calls for preserving the task as the window changes. Godot’s Expand mode preserves aspect ratio while revealing more of the world along the available axis. Anchor the HUD to the resized view and keep touch targets comfortably separated. Android’s accessibility reference uses a minimum 48 dp target, which includes hit area beyond the painted icon. Check the actual target area on each display. These platform checks complement the ad’s visual benchmark.
