## Movement feedback

I reproduced the edge clipping through ordinary screen-touch and drag events in the opening scene, using three isolated emulator layouts. All 24 left/right/top/bottom and corner exercises acquired movement and preserved original touch coordinates on both versions. The full movement ring and displaced knob fit the active viewport and safe area in 0/24 baseline exercises and 24/24 on the repair. The code bounds the drawn feedback using its full extent and keeps the input origin and displacement available to the world.

I reviewed the paired native Fold cover (540 × 1260) and unfolded (840 × 942) captures. The baseline loses the left edge of the rim and its left direction tick. The repair restores both and keeps the thumb offset pointing in the same direction. Character and axe placement are visibly consistent in the paired cover frames. The main matrix independently recorded 82/82 headless assertions and 22/22 native assertions after the repair. The baseline had 25 and six geometric failures, respectively. Touches owned by HUD controls remain excluded from movement. The retained touch-resume regression reproduces the edge problem on the baseline and clears all nine assertions on the repair, including focus loss, two-finger pinch cancellation, and reuse of the same touch ID on return.

## Forest and accessibility

Fresh west, east and south headless routes reached eight distinct waypoints each, held the gameplay camera basis, achieved 2× pinch and kept their profiles writable. Native west and east reached all 16 target waypoints and showed the character through canopy cutaways, including a fold in each direction with renewed touch control. In the west capture I observed the character remain readable at 1×, during the 2× inspection and after the layout change. The west witness recorded wood increasing from 7 to 13, a 5.000-second return deadline for the tracked tree, distant proxy recovery, and the same tree active at the original location on return. The first south native attempt timed out 0.57 units from waypoint seven on a direct approach, completed the next waypoint, and left the first attempt available for inspection. It held the camera, reached 2× and kept the character in view in the submitted frames. A second native run steered through a different clearing at waypoint seven with threats active and completed all eight waypoints. It recorded the same camera basis and 2× pinch, kept the character clear of the HUD in all three sampled positions, saved successfully, and registered live damage by the last checkpoint (30 to 28 HP).

I reviewed native standard and Large HUD settings on the cover and unfolded layouts. All four settings remained inside the screen with the menu title and close control visible. The unfolded muted-damage frame shows “You took damage” above the bottom controls and retains the right-side joystick. The shared accessibility fixture passed 95 headless assertions and the native subset passed all 50 assertions.

## Disposition

The reproduced edge-feedback defect is repaired in the layouts exercised above. The broader suite is 11/12, with the existing extreme storage-bin count assertion failing on both versions. This review covers the observed T01 opening, touch feedback and accessibility scenarios. A physical-device or formal shipping rating was outside this rerun.
