# Guard tower

The previous tower view drew a translucent copy of the player through the deck and the gunner on the southwest return. The updated tower uses the existing gameplay camera and its actual geometry to check when it hides the player. A small cyan arrow identifies the player at those spots. The tower, guard, and loaded bow stay opaque. The independent review scored the opacity and player cue **9.5/10**. The player’s head and gunner can still align from the obstructed position at 2×. The opposite lane shows both actors and the bow separately. The captures use Godot’s desktop compatibility renderer at 720×960 and the standard gameplay camera.
