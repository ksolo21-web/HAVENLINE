# Survivor motion studies

The signed Build 15 replay passed tool identity, actual harvest, and the head and collar clearance checks. The independent visual scores were 9.0 for chopping and 9.3 for mining against a 9.5 target.

The wider preload study kept both tools clear in every sampled pose and increased the axe’s lateral travel from 0.265 m to 0.410 m. The independent replay still found the follow-through too compact and little visible change to pick recovery from the gameplay camera.

The higher preload study reached 0.468 m of vertical axe travel from 0.103 m in the signed build. It passed all eight field checks. The geometry sweep found 12 poses with axe contact at the head and 20 at the collar during wind-up. The outward pick recovery cleared both regions in all 187 sampled poses. Both studies are outside the signed Build 15 source.
