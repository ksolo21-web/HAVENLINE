### What the placement measurements mean

Using the same saved camp and the real scene's paid buildings, gate positions, route heights and service predicates, I sampled the ground on a 7.5 cm grid. At this earned stage, the perimeter increases from 223.2 to 288.0 m². The reachable component of the south gate grew from 128.56 to 183.70 m². Ground with at least two actor diameters of local clearance grew from 100.30 to 146.59 m². The broadest route into the saw's actual activation zone measures 1.03 m for a 0.62 m actor. The counter measures 2.32 m. These estimates subtract mesh-deck width and building footprints. The touch-controlled player reached the working faces of the saw and the counter through the paid yard. The forester completed five successive machine, store and counter approaches with zero blocked steps.
