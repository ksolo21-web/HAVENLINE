# Mining the edge of the bank

[Play the mining repair at the normal gameplay camera](Mining-from-the-working-edge.mp4) · [Normal camera comparison](Quarry-comparison-1x.jpg) · [Pinch camera comparison](Quarry-comparison-2x.jpg)

In Android Build 11’s field scene, the northern and western thumbstick approaches registered one hit and left the starter quarry standing. Each strike had reduced the entire bank by 18 per cent, including the visible surface and walking collider. At the outer edge of the stroke, the automatic tool selector could then switch to the axe while the character was still alongside the rock. The eastern comparison registered zero hits in the same observation window. [Recorded contacts on the three approaches](Touch-approach-comparison.json) · [Stalled bank at normal zoom](Build11/stalled-1x.jpg)

The new mine keeps its working surface through two full contacts. A new seam appears on each accepted strike. On the third, four sections cut from the original snow-capped mesh separate, the walking collider clears, and three distinct loose stones appear on the snow. Each loose stone is a bundle of ten. Collecting all three raises the HUD from zero to 30 stone. This also applies to the working stone, metal ore, slate and coal farther out in World One. Each bank returned after exactly 300 playable physics ticks and supported another harvest. [Five-bank depletion, pickup, streaming and cold-restart checks](Mineral-yields-and-five-second-returns.json)

The starter quarry occupies open snow southwest of the south gate once the camp is paid. A disposable copy of Build 10’s earned opening kept its original one strike on that deposit. The character ran through the south gate and made the remaining two contacts. An expanded camp with all paid connections retained a clear approach and required three contacts on its intact quarry.
