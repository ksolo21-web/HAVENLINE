# Camp furnace

In Build 11 the central furnace briefly flashed when it was bought. After the reveal its small firebed used a static texture, the opening had no moving flame, and the vent had no draft. A returning injured player could recover throughout the camp, but the recovery feedback gave no indication of its heat source. [Build 11 at normal zoom](Build11-normal.jpg) · [Build 11 at pinch zoom](Build11-pinch.jpg)

The recovered stone housing now contains a live arched firebox and a bright upper bed under a drifting plume. Both effects are fitted to the openings measured in the original stone model. Firelight reaches the chamber and nearby ground. The existing positional fire loop follows the furnace. The paid hearth supplies the existing camp-wide recovery. A successful recovery pulse raises the firelight briefly, shows “Hearth +3 HP” over the character and updates the health bar. A final pulse shows the amount actually regained. At full health the fire continues burning. [Gameplay at normal zoom](Normal-gameplay.jpg) · [Gameplay at 2×](Pinch-gameplay.jpg) · [Reference beside gameplay](Reference-and-played-furnace.jpg)

The owner's recording B shows the vessel appear around 6.30 s and the floor thaw by 6.60 s. At 8 and 10 s its bright crown and hot lower opening are still visible after the purchase. The selected Havenline sheet supplies the masonry, arch shape and soft exhaust. The paid plot and camp-wide HP recovery are the trial's game rules. The 3 HP per second rate comes from the current Level 1 tuning.
