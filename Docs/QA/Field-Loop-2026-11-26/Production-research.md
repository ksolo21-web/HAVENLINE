# How the references informed the playable opening

## Measure what the player sees

In the locked portrait recording B, a field character fills about 12.6% of the short screen dimension and a camp character about 10.7%. The shoreline recording A uses a larger character. We measured from visible head to boots, excluding the load, and measured the orbit from the character’s center to the axe head. Comparing these proportions survives changes in capture resolution. Comparing the entire frame at phone size exposed the blocked quarry and an apparent extra steak that were easy to miss close up. [Frame measurements](References/Locked-field-reference.md) · [Independent camera review](References/Second-field-review.md) · [Corrections](References/Field-camera-corrections.jpg)

The field uses one following camera for walking, attack, mining, purchases and pinch. The game's touch handler tracks contact identities so a two-finger pinch and a one-finger walk can share the surface. The review uses 2× as the maximum because the supplied recordings do not establish a pinch limit. Keeping the same camera and framing the player on the short axis lets the same native-input route expose occlusion and readability in both mobile orientations. [Android multi-touch guidance](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/touch-and-input/gestures/multi) · [Actual field controls](https://github.com/ksolo21-web/HAVENLINE/blob/994ace861fbf132c5924bacc320f5608d75d9187/HavenlineLevel1/docs/Field-Loop.md)

## Make weight visible during changes of direction

A planted foot can roll from heel to toe while its point of support stays in place. A stationary ankle test misses a sliding sole or a knee folding through the other leg. The rig review therefore sampled the deformed boots, opposing shins and support motion during starts, braking, reversal and turns. Walking and running share a contact phase and follow actual movement on the ground. The resulting measurements cover 4,000 poses. The evidence retains the remaining wide swing in a sharp turn so Alfred can judge it from the playing camera. [Kovar, Schreiner and Gleicher on footplant constraints](https://graphics.cs.wisc.edu/Papers/2002/KSG02/) · [Johansen’s locomotion research](https://runevision.com/thesis/) · [Rig and gait review](Character/Independent-QA/V12-16b75a22/Review.md)

The player’s tool motion has a different reference. B shows a near-horizontal orbit arriving from behind the hero as well as in front. A physical tool head at the recorded radius, period and upgrade spacing allows a moving actor to earn a tree, stone or enemy hit on the same sweep the viewer sees. The stance stays legible beneath the tool and carried material. Survivor chopping has its own reference and remains a separate production task. [Orbit, actor and loose-item measurements](References/Independent-field-review.md) · [Frozen movement and contact tests](Gameplay-Evidence/Frozen-regressions.json)

## Author the closest view and measure the busiest view

The owner’s turnarounds determine visible material identity. End grain and twisted rope identify three bound logs, fractured gray surfaces distinguish stone, and a pale fat edge surrounds a red steak. When those units sit on a filled storage plot, the amount the player sees must also match the amount they can spend. Removing the large matching picture while a bay is occupied restored that count. At normal zoom the silhouettes and grouping do most of this work. At maximum pinch, the bevels, surface finish and joins also matter. [Android geometry guidance](https://developer.android.com/games/optimize/geometry) · [Texture guidance](https://developer.android.com/games/optimize/textures) · [Earned inventory comparison](Gameplay-Evidence/Meat-plot-before-after.jpg) · [Wood detail](Gameplay-Evidence/Wood-end-grain.jpg)

Repeated geometry is also measurable. Regrouping the timber geometry by its existing material reduced a tested paid view from 1,325 to 305 draw calls while preserving its 405,150 rendered primitives. The 4K stills use native pixels. Real-time delivery at 60 or 120 Hz separately requires frames to arrive within 16.67 or 8.33 milliseconds on the target hardware, including after it warms up. Native pixel dimensions, simulated physics rates and the time base on a recorded movie each answer a different question. ## Time transfers and sound against player actions

The source shows separate payments arriving at the pad, a green fill, furnace ignition and a spreading floor. The trial ties each material flight to an actual debit. The last arrival completes the build, and the receipt records when the floor starts to change. Sound follows the corresponding events and the score changes with field, threat and hearth. The reviewers checked the actual mix with the game playing, including quieter sounds occurring under music. An isolated cue and a comparison with the source recording answer different questions. The B soundtrack was unavailable for that second comparison.
