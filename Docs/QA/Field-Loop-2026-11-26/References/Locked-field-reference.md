# Field, delivery and upgrade measurements

The primary measurement is **H**, the standing hero's visible hood-to-sole distance excluding cargo. Width is horizontal screen width unless explicitly labeled long axis. Depth is projected screen height. Perspective, pose and partial occlusion give most bounds 15–25% uncertainty. These estimates come from the original 1080 × 2520 PNGs after removing the phone/video surround at y≈273–2192. The detail sheets have an independent crop and scale, so every ratio uses a hero from the same shot. **Recording-time sequence.** The preserved original dense sheets contain 10 seek samples per second. At B5.4 the build pad is gray, B5.7–6.1 documents progressive green fill, and B6.2 shows the heater. A bright flash follows at B6.3–6.4, with the brown surface spreading across the snow at B6.5–6.6. In B13.0–13.4 the loaded character approaches the upgrade while the world scrolls. B13.5–13.8 shows separate green notes leaving the load. A yellow-white burst arrives at B13.9. Multiple rotating heads are visible at B14.1–14.2. The broader overview at nominal B14 shows three separated heads. A12.5–13.0 shows green notes paying onto the rod pad; at A13.1 one remains visible and at A13.2 the orange intake is appearing. Event edges have at least ±0.1 recorded seconds of sampling uncertainty. These are edited recording positions. They establish staged payments and visible transformations without establishing universal cooldowns or simulation rates.
