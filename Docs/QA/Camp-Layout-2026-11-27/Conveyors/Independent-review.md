# Independent review of dispatch and counter service

The machinery developer and the separate rig/gameplay reviewer compared the frozen shipping scene with Havenline's selected straight-conveyor art and the locked food-production footage. The post-repair camera recording used native Godot 4.7.2 on Linux, OpenGL compatibility and Mesa llvmpipe at 1280 × 720, 12 captured frames per second. The player remained still during the automated transactions.

**Automated dispatch and customer exchange earned 9.5/10 in this review.** The reviewer decoded 27 dispatch frames. The warehouse doors were initially closed, opened within the first half-second, then closed again around 1.7 seconds. Actual stock crossed the open central threshold. In 28 close customer-service frames, a single roast traveled from the cooker, reached the customer, and the green banknote appeared as the displayed money balance changed from 1 to 2. The service capture includes the game audio. [Play the sequence](Camp-conveyors-and-customer.mp4)

**Conveyor hardware appearance remains open.** The working stock reads small and passes over exposed ground. At 16 seconds in recording A, a larger load and its physical conveyor are prominent from the gameplay camera. The current archive straight-module sheet informed the floor icon. The dedicated final line art is still outstanding. The camera framing at pinch zoom can place the HUD over the far end of the queue.
