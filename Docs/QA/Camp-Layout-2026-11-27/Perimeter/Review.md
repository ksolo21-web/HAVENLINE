## Measured behavior

- **Gate travel.** The matrix covers the two gates, all three purchased perimeters, entry and exit, both sides of the opening, and seven held approaches. Build 8 stopped on every one of the 24 shallow post approaches. The dedicated frozen replay completed 168/168, with zero closed-fence crossings or death resets. The independent full-suite run reached its 1.65 m clearance marker within 132 ticks on 167/168. On the remaining run the character had already crossed, and a wolf stopped it 1.18 m beyond the east seam. Median traversal took 49 ticks. The largest movement at the seam was 0.104 m. A wolf physically blocked one approach for 46 ticks before the runner passed. The hinges never held during that pause. The Build 9 steering adjustment only engages for input aimed at the opening. Each physical leaf still checks the player, worker and nearby wolf before swinging. From rest, full opening took 0.700 s at 30 Hz and 0.683 s at 60 and 120 Hz. - **Contested east exit.** Two follow-up runs held the same corner input for 300 ticks against four genuine wolves, the production cap. By tick 300 both runners were over 14 m outside. An artificial six-wolf stress run wedged a runner against a leaf for the full five seconds. - **Orbit.** Both full-size tools turn along nearby boards and open leaves, with no scale change. Both retained their 0.89-second cycle in the fixture. Across 3,456 sweeps from the actor to the corners of the authored weapon bounds, zero crossed solid timber. The axe and pickaxe hit reachable resource triangles alongside the fence and through the open east gate. An otherwise identical target behind the closed leaves or solid fence received zero contacts. The wall-facing arc is deliberately compressed when the available space is narrow. At 0.40 s in the 2× clip, the axe passes behind the player in camera projection and is briefly harder to read.
