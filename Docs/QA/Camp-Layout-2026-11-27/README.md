## What to try

- Drag to run, pinch to zoom, and leave through either gate. Approach the east post diagonally with the stick held. The gate opens on approach, and the orbit deploys outside. Try the same route back into camp. [Before and after on the same approach](Perimeter/East-gate-held-diagonal.mp4) · [East gate at pinch zoom](Perimeter/East-gate-pinch.mp4)
- After buying equipment, tap **Move**, select a building, choose its new footprint and tap **Place here**. The preview shows blocked positions and protects walking and service space. The counter slides to another north fence bay. The tower stays on the threat side. [Move the paid cooker by touch](Layout/Move-paid-cooker-by-touch.mp4)
- After paying for the cooker, tap **Automate**. **Automate meal service** costs 100 wood and 60 stone and sends cooked food to the customer counter. **Connect storehouse supply** costs 160 wood and 120 stone and feeds owned workshops from organized stock. Walk near the selected purchase mat to pay. The worker and goods follow a machine after a move. The optional upgrades currently show moving goods across the floor. Their visible rails await the owner's conveyor renders. [Watch the delivery and sale with game audio](Conveyors/Camp-conveyors-and-customer.mp4) · [Portrait purchase menu](Conveyors/Choose-automation.jpg)
- Rescue the forester and leave the player away from the work. The forester can gather, fund ordinary construction, operate equipment and serve a meal. The empty-bank test reached the first home shelf delivery at 217.95 simulation seconds. The player and forester share the 6.25 m/s top run speed. The forester can carry 500 of each of the six resources. The player passed 1,000, reached 25,000 of each in a stress test and retained the balances after a cold start.
