# Play the Fold update

Open the [private Havenline Android playtest page](https://havenline-level1-android-playtest.ksolo21.chatgpt.site) on the phone and tap **Download Android APK**. Open the download and choose **Update** when Android recognizes the existing trial. It uses the same app ID and signing certificate as the previous trial. Installing over the previous APK preserves the app’s local camp progress. An old 4/4 health bar becomes 30/30, with injured characters retaining their approximate health percentage.

| Action | Phone control |
| --- | --- |
| Run | Drag one finger on open ground in the direction you want to move. A short deliberate drag runs at the same top speed as a long drag. Lift to stop. |
| Pass a gate | Run toward the opening from either side. The leaves open automatically and wait until the opening clears. |
| Fight, chop, or mine | Run outside camp and bring the target into the rotating weapon’s path. Its full length deals damage. The camp deactivates the weapon while you are inside. |
| Zoom | Pinch with two fingers from the normal gameplay view up to 2×. Lift your fingers after folding or rotating to start a new gesture. |
| Change graphics quality | Tap the quality control to switch between Ultra and Standard. |

The project opens in the Level 1 field. An existing trial continues from its last saved progress. QA used separate disposable profiles on the build machine.
