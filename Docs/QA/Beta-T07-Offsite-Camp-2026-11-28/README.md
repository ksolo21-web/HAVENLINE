# Havenline Beta T07 camp operation review

The snow truck's compass follows a driveable approach to the garage. When the player dismounts, walking guidance resumes. A payable plot turns green as the player settles into the interaction, before a parcel leaves the pack. The fill follows actual receipts. Moving away pauses new payments, clears the ready cue, and leaves confirmed payment progress visible.

A rescued survivor can haul raw materials into unconnected workshops, pick up finished planks and blocks, stock the meal counter, bank customer cash, and repair an affordable owned site. A conveyor purchase requires the player's active selection. The worker checks that selection again before spending and can resume an interrupted delivery without repeating the debit.

The offsite scenario places the player beyond the raid system's unattended radius and stages an unlocked camp with stock. All 13 checks cover the worker's repairs, production deliveries, customer sales, cash storage, conveyor authorization, and conserved save state. An additional route uses actual hostile contact to interrupt a plot transfer and verifies that the same parcel resumes. The source also passed ten regression suites.
