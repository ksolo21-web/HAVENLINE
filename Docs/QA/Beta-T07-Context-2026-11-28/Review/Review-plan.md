Validate stable identity, determinism under reversed candidate order, facing/relevance ordering, compatibility with T06, and no extra payment/damage/carry authority. |
| Urgency, roles and save | A genuine urgent enemy/rescue can preempt lower-priority focus with readable feedback; away movement clears contextual work. Start fresh and reopen an interrupted paid route with canonical progress reconstructed and conserved goods. |
| Truck field contact | Center, left and right approaches to a rooted pine, bank and live wolf. Use actual travel to contact, compare a tangent miss, cooldown and boundaries. Observe the mounted twin mechanisms, source damage, one depletion, yield in truck cargo and intact no-contact objects. |
