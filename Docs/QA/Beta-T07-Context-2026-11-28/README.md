# Havenline Beta T07

T07 uses the published T06 build as its base. The player still uses one on-foot joystick. Nearby actions share one focus, and movement pauses the entry to contextual work. Ordinary actions settle briefly before they can start. The warehouse starts unloading as soon as the player stops there, keeping the existing transfer timing. Ground pickup, orbiting axe contact, and unattended work stay automatic.

The director selects a recipient and reports distance, capability, readiness, and progress. The existing routes, collision checks and journal own physical contact and receipts. The same choice drives the objective prompt and the T06 preparation gesture. The active focus is recreated at runtime, with no saved token.

## Snow truck

The published T06 truck already covered the space between the horizontal blades. Tests verify centered chops, mining and wolf damage, plus off-center contact, out-of-reach rejection, cooldown and actual driven mining. The truck now backs clear of a pedestrian gate corner and can line up on either side of the garage opening. The rear stop keeps its full visible envelope within the bay while allowing the shutter to close with the driver aboard. The player can reach the vehicle from the open camp side using distance to the hull. The original models and armor, damage and cargo rules are retained.
