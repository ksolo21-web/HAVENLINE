Work surfaces and output trays sit on the clear, warm camp floor. Resource-side fixtures can cross the perimeter. [Six reference views](Camp-production-reference.jpg)

The footage directly identifies fish and crop lines. It gives no wood-to-stone transfer, direct wood or stone delivery to a counter, production duration in game seconds, offline policy, or exact width in metres for Havenline’s aisles. The saw, stone processor and meat cooker are Havenline adaptations. A short local feed and collection tray for each machine follows the observed pattern.
