# North counter and east ballista

The independent gameplay review scored the north counter **9.5/10** and the staffed ballista **9.5/10** on revision `3fad812b384335bd8ca9b5e47dc9d9557110eb4a`. Both passed. The reviewer watched the complete turn at both zooms, checked the two obstructed camera positions and the actual release and hit, and confirmed counter separation from both sides and after restoration.

The north counter now blocks the player across its entire purchased fence bay. A returning character whose coordinates lie inside the counter footprint is placed on the ground on the same side. In the older build the player could traverse the counter at board height. Reopening an embedded position could leave the character standing on the service surface.

The regression drives the real character collider against both faces at seven lateral positions, at ground and board height. It repeats at the original and relocated paid bays, then reopens saved positions on both sides. Build 12 passed 30 of 60 cases. The revised camp passed 60 of 60. All 24 crossings at the two real gates passed at the three perimeter sizes. The counter suite exercised exterior customer queuing, cashier movement, meal transfer and single-order payment. [Collision results](Gameplay-regressions.json) · [Individual counter approaches](After-physics/Counter-collision.json) · [Filmed exchange at normal zoom](../Economy/Counter-normal.mp4) · [Filmed exchange at pinch zoom](../Economy/Counter-pinch.mp4)

The paid ballista keeps its painted cabin and deck opaque when the player walks behind them. A depth-tested effect outlines the occluded part of the player's own model. The operator turns on a dedicated timber tread behind the gun and winds the visible eccentric handle with the right hand. The other hand holds the fixed stock control. The tread, mounting struts and winding hardware use textured parts fitted from the original Meshy tower.

The before-and-after rig diagnosis uses the actual field operator, paid weapon and native transforms. One full turn, two reloads and 18 additional steep aim and recoil poses produced 415 samples. Both gloved meshes touched their intended handles in all 415. The bow, bolt, turntable and supports cleared the head, torso, pelvis and legs throughout. The closest soles measured 0–2 mm from the tread at the sampled bearings. The native gameplay regression also verified launch from the loaded quarrel, travel before impact, damage on contact and reloading.
