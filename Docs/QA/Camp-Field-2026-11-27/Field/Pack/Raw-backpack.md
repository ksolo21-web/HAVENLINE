# Carrying 52,000 units

A full wood, stone and meat load now fits behind the keeper. In matched 480 × 720 photographs, the old stacks reached 7.68 metres at normal zoom and 4.07 metres at pinch zoom. The same load now reaches 1.85 metres at both. At pinch zoom, the highest visible packet moved from 12% to 30% of the way down the screen, clear of the objective. The 52,000 units stay in their respective stacks as represented quantities. [Matched before and after photographs](Raw-backpack-comparison.jpg)

The warehouse approach started with 17,340 units of wood, 17,330 of stone and 17,330 of meat. All three exact balances transferred to the warehouse and survived a cold reload. In a separate matched run, native touch moved the keeper 2.31 metres. His position and both boot positions were identical to the preceding build on each of 43 physics ticks. The carry tests also exercised each of the 63 nonempty combinations of six materials, small individual stacks and up to one million bundles per material. [Depot transfer](Field-route.log) · [Carry and motion measurements](Raw-backpack-audit.json) · [Carry tests](qa_field_pack_bounds.log)

The matched photographs use the Level 1 field scene at 480 × 720, after three physics ticks at each zoom, with Godot 4.7.2 and desktop OpenGL software rendering. In an independent review, reference_check scored the two views **9.9/10** for legible character, HUD and separated materials under this load. The footage at 28 seconds in reference B and the selected Havenline stock turnarounds establish the physical carry appearance. Grouping 52,000 units into a keeper-sized pack is a game design decision. Tower implemented the correction. The migrated-inventory run initially showed two coal, three slate and two metal bundles at the right of the screen. At that position the backpack was reserving the height of an objective card on the opposite side. It now shows each of the earned three coal, four slate and five metal alongside the existing wood and stone. The original migration, stacking, mineral-art, mining and storage tests pass. In a separate rendered reproduction, collecting two meat bundles brought all six types onto the same rack. The normal view shows all 16 packets.
