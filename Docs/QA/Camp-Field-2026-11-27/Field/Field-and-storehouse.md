Build 12 wrote the complete inventory once for each pickup and again when its animation ended. A disposable paid camp with 5,450 prior receipts and an empty pack took 1.99 seconds in a single physics frame when 40 nearby meat and stone bundles were collected. Another 32 bundles sat 2 m away, outside the old 1.38 m collection radius. At 72 loose bundles, each new spawn erased the oldest live reward. These tests reproduced the hitch and the lost drops on desktop using historical saves and synthetic stock. The owner’s individual phone journal was unavailable. Collection now extends 2.35 m over clear ground. Each eligible token goes into its own material balance. Large groups share up to three moving models per material, and a batch requires one inventory checkpoint. A forest or camp pickup still checks the physical path to the player. Closed timber blocks a transfer. Loose uncollected rewards keep their existing 100 seconds of played time. A newer save from another writer leaves pending pickups on the ground and preserves the newer file. [Cold launch, concurrency and wall checks](Grouping-fence-transfer-and-save-checks.json)

An ordinary 52-bundle load still sends each model into the storehouse. At 5,200 bundles, the warehouse sent 12 representative packets in four overlapping groups. The completed groups settled at physics frames 17, 29, 41 and 53, and a cold reader verified the balances immediately after each group. The warmed desktop stress test reduced 63 inventory checkpoints to four across this visit. Physical Android frame times remain unmeasured. [Owner transfer frames and source art](Source-notes.json) · [Raw transfer checks](Grouping-fence-transfer-and-save-checks.json)

The fully paid camp fixture includes three orbiting tools, a rescued worker’s first delivery, paid equipment, a served meal, and an earlier owl hit. The runner harvested the same quarry four times in 23 seconds and collected 12 stone bundles. A separate fixture reopened with all 128 scattered field tokens accounted for. The warehouse fixture accounted for 52,000 raw units across its active shelves and production line after a cold reopen. [Progressed camp and reopened receipts](Progressed-camp-and-cold-reloads.json)

A separate regression followed both individual yields from first strike to inventory across five bank faces. Two of the remote pine’s three wood receipts reached the pack automatically while the third remained on the snow. [Regrowth and typed receipts](Regrowth-and-typed-receipts.json)

Entering the starter warehouse through the center now unloads 10 wood and 20 meat and leaves four stone on the runner. Passing its adjacent upgrade pad preserves those resources, including when the runner is turning under held input. Stopping beside the upgrade starts payment, and leaving settles only the packets already released. [Touch route, cold reopen and interrupted purchase](On-foot-upgrade-and-loading.json)

In a fully expanded camp, a wolf previously held the runner for 84 physics ticks just outside the open east gate. The runner can now brush past a hunter in the open passage while it moves to the shoulder. The same diagonal exit reached 1.74 m beyond the sill in 49 ticks. A second run with an alpha parked outside recorded a four-HP bite after the runner cleared the fence. All 168 ordinary gate approaches passed with the live pack active.
