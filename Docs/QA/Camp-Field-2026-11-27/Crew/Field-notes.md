# Survivor and owl field evidence

## Survivor

In a paid camp with connected production lines, the forester delivers the first harvested wood at 14.13 seconds. On a later route he gathers 120 wood and 120 stone across multiple trees and rock harvests before walking back to the storage house. His arms stay free and his back rack keeps the two materials in separate rows. The complete wood-and-stone transfer takes 0.85 seconds. Each arrival credits the matching shelf. The stationary player’s balance stays unchanged. At the same camera position and under the same field conditions, the previous build peaked at 30 units per trip, with zero mixed loads.

Each raw material has a separate 500-unit limit for the survivor. The capacity test fills each of the six materials to 490 and runs the ordinary pickup with two extra bundles per type. One 10-unit bundle per type lands on the rack and the other stays on the snow. The saved balances reopen and the six loads discharge to the matching shelves. When a customer is waiting, a survivor can give one manual kitchen order priority during each work rotation. The test moves the storage house while wood is on the way, moves the cooker while the survivor carries meat and moves the counter while he holds the cooked order. The worker serves the guest 31.62 seconds into the scenario. The previous rotation took 232.83 seconds. Other checks exercise early rescue, plot buying and manual supply to the saw and quarry.

## Owl

After buying the central hearth, the player can approach the roost southeast of the south gate and spend 60 meat. The worker contract costs 80 wood at a separate southwest plot. A test walks through the south gate and pays half the roost price, then restarts and delivers the other half. A second restart reopens the paid roost. An archived version 9 save with an employed forester and a cooked order in hand restores the owl earned under the previous combined contract and retains the same holdings.

The field patrol recorded four talon contacts over 13.62 seconds of monitored gameplay. The owl guards the exterior while the owner stands inside, exits with the player, intercepts a wolf after the player loses two HP, returns to its perch as the player heals and engages a threat at the other gate while the player stays inside. Damage uses the moving talons and the enemy’s actual triangle surface. A separate 21-check articulation test examines the folded and flying Meshy rigs, bank, rake and recovery. The independent field critic scored the owl 9.5/10 after reviewing the safe gate strike in the normal camera and the separate boss encounter at pinch zoom.
