# T06 forward plan for Midnight Man, Briar Vale and their vehicles

Add **Midnight Man** and **Briar Vale** as playable leads alongside the existing four. Include **Tumbler** with Midnight Man and **Winterbriar** with Briar Vale for design, rider fitting and interaction evidence. This adds work to the T06 plan. The [Character 1 frozen scope](FROZEN_SCOPE.md), [recorded completion](verified-completion.json) and the T59–T61 work for the other original leads keep their original scope. The two new leads and vehicles are planned concepts awaiting runtime work and independent review. Pairing identifies the fit and motion to test. Unlock levels, acquisition and character-exclusive use are open.

## Character designs and actions

| Playable lead | Art and rig focus | Actions to plan and review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Midnight Man** | Hooded winter armor, heavy split cape, copper lining and mountain crest. Keep the face, hands and weapon readable at the normal gameplay camera. Check cape clearance through strikes, turns and boarding. | Shadow Batarang automatically targets eligible enemies, trees or mineable rocks. Check anticipation, hand release, flight, contact or miss, then recovery. Preserve **Sweep** (jab → cross → sweeping kick), **Breakthrough** (advancing punch → rising knee → heavy stomp) and the proposed **Rebound** (side-step kick → spinning backfist → auto-targeted batarang). Throw trigger, cadence, target priority, range, damage, controls and the final Rebound variant are open. |
| **Briar Vale** | Adult plant sorceress with copper-red hair, emerald leaf armor, living vines and a winterberry crown. Match the supplied silhouette and use restrained hair, vine and clothing follow-through. | Briar fights at range with **Poison Kiss**, **Vine Lash** and **Root Rupture**. Target selection is automatic for every cast. Poison Kiss homes in and applies damage over time, Vine Lash can disrupt, push or sweep as defined by the combo, and Root Rupture telegraphs before damage and a brief pin. Preserve **Deadly Bloom** (Poison Kiss → Vine Lash → Root Rupture), **Briar Trap** (Root Rupture → Vine Lash → Poison Kiss) and **Thorn Retreat** (Vine Lash → Poison Kiss with a backstep → Root Rupture). The first-move/follow-up control and valid retargeting are design proposals. |

These kits apply to their respective playable leads. Preserve the standard lead's orbiting axes and survivors' two-handed tool interactions. [Midnight Man design](https://github.com/ksolo21-web/HAVENLINE/blob/4732dc7ba04825ab2a721a855055284c8810c259/Docs/Design/CharacterTurnarounds/2026-11-28/midnight-man/README.md) · [Briar Vale design](https://github.com/ksolo21-web/HAVENLINE/blob/4732dc7ba04825ab2a721a855055284c8810c259/Docs/Design/CharacterTurnarounds/2026-11-28/briar-vale/README.md)

## Vehicle designs and rider interaction

| Pairing | Vehicle concept | T06 motion and contact evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Midnight Man and Tumbler** | Armored World 1 resource hauler with twin horizontal cutters and a rear cargo hatch above the turbine. Cutters contact eligible enemies, trees and all mineable rocks and obey paid-camp cutter safety. The in-game volley acquires up to **10 distinct eligible enemies**, fires only on **Launch** input and starts a shared **3-second cooldown** after a successful volley. Each missile homes on its assigned target. | Prove approach, entry, seated reach, grip and driving poses, stop and exit. Keep the cape, limbs and cargo presentation clear of the vehicle and nearby geometry. Show the rider-to-cutter and rider-to-volley transitions at gameplay scale. |
| **Briar Vale and Winterbriar** | Two-wheel snow scout with emerald leaf armor, a copper frame, studded tires, a violet headlamp and two cargo panniers. Winterbriar is an unarmed scout. Mounted Vine Lash and Root Rupture are proposals for Briar's own abilities. | Prove mount and dismount, saddle fit, hands on the bars, boots on the footrests, steering lean, stops and both wheels making believable contact on snow and slopes. Check hair, vines and loaded panniers clear the wheels, steering and rider. Test mounted cast poses and reach if that proposal is selected. |

Tumbler and Winterbriar add distinct roles alongside the Snow Truck. Their cost, power source, unlock and final balance remain open. Winterbriar's pannier capacity and docking are also open. Vehicle owners verify typed cargo, exactly-once unloading and reload for both designs, plus Tumbler's inside/outside wreck rules and explicit Launch input. Set capacity and power from each new vehicle's approved design. [World 1 vehicle descriptions](https://github.com/ksolo21-web/HAVENLINE/blob/772cabd3613345bda15434c7118443359be11dea/Docs/Design/WorldVehicles/2026-11-28/README.md) · [Existing World 1 planning reconciliation](https://github.com/ksolo21-web/HAVENLINE/blob/1e04d0dee0367c0dcfbb222047de7b7ef6b6b0ee/Docs/Design/World1Upgrades/2026-11-28/roster-additions/tumbler-midnight-man/README.md)

## Delivery and acceptance

1. Use the pinned renders as Meshy inputs for the new models. Validate silhouettes, size against existing people, equipment and terrain, and distinguish the two riders and vehicles in the normal gameplay view. Bind each actual asset to its source sheet and record native asset identity.
2. In T06, prove each lead's rig, grounded gait, starts, stops, turns, loaded movement and relevant contact poses. Capture complete throws, casts and combos, including miss, interruption and recovery, at gameplay speed and in slow multi-angle review. Inspect feet, knees, hands, armor, hair, cape, vines and ride transitions. Bind full captures to the exact new candidate and apply the existing **9.9 minimum for final reference-sensitive dimensions**, with stronger exact-10 gates where applicable. [Reference acceptance standard](../REFERENCE_ACCEPTANCE_STANDARD.md)
3. Keep automatic input or deliberate actions with T07, cargo and handoff with T08, harvest outcomes with T09, and targeting and damage with T21. Coordinate World 1 vehicle implementation and validation through T32 and T53. Check Tumbler's cutters across all mineable types, the volley at 0, 1, 10 and more than 10 eligible targets, and blocked unloading and save/reload. Check Winterbriar on slopes, narrow routes and in cargo transfers. T06 supplies motion and rider fit for these tests while those tasks retain their system ownership. [Vehicle economy context](https://github.com/ksolo21-web/HAVENLINE/blob/772cabd3613345bda15434c7118443359be11dea/Docs/Production/VEHICLE_ECONOMY.md)

Follow the [GitHub pre-task check](../../../AGENTS.md#check-github-before-starting-a-task) before implementing. Use the current beta for gameplay and accessibility review. Include integrated performance and the playable app at the next applicable ten-task milestone, followed by owner sign-off.
