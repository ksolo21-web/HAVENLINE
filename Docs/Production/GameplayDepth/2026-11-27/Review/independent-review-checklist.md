Scope: source-grounded full-game audit and forward planning requirements. No runtime, gameplay, animation, balance, device or build approval.

## Mandatory checks

1. **Source identity and truthful status.** State the exact audited plan/source and distinguish committed requirements, historical receipts, current live build evidence and unverified implementation. Do not use task status APPROVED as current gameplay proof.
2. **Complete question coverage.** Explain what the player is trying to protect/avoid, all relevant kinds of stakes, survivors and all seven pets, full state/transition animation, and all known resources/currencies plus unknown future additions.
3. **Evidence/proposal boundary.** Separate source-observed rules, the user's new explicit directives, and proposed failure/economy/ability/tuning decisions. Do not derive unobserved rules or timing from reference footage or renders.
4. **Player stakes contract.** Each proposed or existing threat has a readable trigger, warning, player choice/counterplay, concrete consequence, recovery, protected progress/assets and owner task. Consequences must matter without unsupported fixed loss percentages or irreversible choices masquerading as approved design.
5. **Fair recovery and meaningful pressure.** No proposal contradicts spend-blind difficulty, achievable $0 completion, permanent transparent VIP, one primary premium currency, or preserved advertised value. Rule out softlocks/negative spirals, involuntary premium spending, and fabricated permissions for offline attacks/permanent loss. Distinguish harmful friction from meaningful strategic tradeoffs.
6. **Human task parity.** Human survivors can eventually assist every relevant player work task using training/equipment/capacity; role specialization improves performance rather than silently forbidding the user's requested broad assistance. Include acquisition, mixed-cargo hauling/deposit, production, service, build/repair/plot funding, combat/defense, rescue/healing, and later-region transport where applicable.
7. **Delegated purchase authority and conservation.** Survivor plot purchasing explicitly operates through player-delegated choices/budgets and the same authoritative eligibility/cost/exactly-once transaction rules. Parallel workers cannot overpay, double-unlock, spend reserved stock twice, lose cargo on cancellation/save, or spend premium/real money autonomously.
8. **Observable intelligence and failure behavior.** Jobs have reservation ownership, contextual priorities, safe interruption, carrying custody, route/congestion recovery, stuck behavior, threat response, offscreen/save behavior and understandable intent. No claim that unspecified 'AI' is automatically smart. Player remains useful and automation cannot erase the intended game loop.
9. **Seven distinct useful companions.** Dog,wolf,fox,owl,lion,tiger,bear have species-fitting physical jobs plus distinct utility/strength/limitations. No human-tool fallback or seven identical stat buffs. Every pet must have a worthwhile use beyond cosmetic follow behavior; proposals stay labeled.
10. **Animation completeness.** Coverage names full cycles and transitions for actors, equipment and hostiles: idle/approach/setup/work/contact/recovery/carry/delivery, interrupted/blocked/damaged/defeated and relevant empty/full/restart states. State changes and impacts agree with authoritative gameplay; species/role differences are visible rather than renamed generic clips.
11. **Motion/audio review evidence.** Require actual playback, meaningful angles, gameplay-scale readability and contact/clipping checks; audio requires listening and meaningful cue/state linkage. Avoid demanding a unique noise/clip for every tiny event when deliberate reuse/silence preserves clarity; no frames-only full-motion approval.
12. **Exhaustive resource identity coverage.** Inventory all source-identified registry IDs, current foundation items, named render-backed materials/processed variants and currency categories without turning render variants into automatically approved recipes/currencies. Distinguish alias/family/variant/new-ID decisions and record unknown future resources as unresolved.
13. **Resource/currency value chain.** Each active item/currency needs acquisition/source, practical player decision and sink, relevant progression/later usefulness, replacement/surplus policy, carrying/storage rules and task owner. Every recipe must consume/produce coherently. No arbitrary universal sellability, infinite circular exchanges or useless currency inflation. Primary premium currency remains singular and separated from earned physical stock.
14. **Task integration and authority.** Requirements map to authentic T01–T70 owners, build/gate tasks and whole-game acceptance, preserving IDs/dependencies/lifecycle/ownership/earlier frozen evidence. T71–T80 remain undefined. Proposed new breadth must not silently activate later runtime work or merge gameplay branches.
15. **Executable planning checks and retained findings.** Any new validator/generator is tested against real contract files and meaningful negatives, preserves old schemas/requirements where needed, rejects missing coverage rather than auto-passing, and labels structural checks as plan consistency only. Independent findings and repair evidence are retained.
16. **Publication boundary and final answer accuracy.** Freeze the exact reviewed candidate, provide an honest minimum score with every mandatory check resolved, and distinguish this plan approval from a game quality or implementation approval. Parent separately verifies GitHub publication and reports the actual remaining runtime/tuning decisions.
