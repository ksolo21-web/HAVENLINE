Get explicit production scope decisions for river/perimeter, player orbit vs held tools, changed camera/device framing, and the role of mobile equipment and conveyors. 2. **Resolve the visible upstream issues first.** Retest T03–T05 with the final fence/gate/prop assets and actor on one normal-camera route. Reopen the affected T06 gait/worker pace checks based on the latest owner report. In parallel, verify T07 context ranking and T08/T09 all-resource custody/contacts without altering the working playtest.
3. **Prove the two layers of progression.** Reconcile T10 transaction behavior with its reusable production framework, then prove T11 actual purchased construction and relocation against it. For conveyors, require one real order from cooked output via finite stock to a staffed buffer, physical service and one payment, including full-buffer hold and reload.
