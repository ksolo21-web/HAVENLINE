## Most important corrections to the plan

1. **Keep the opening independent of later systems.** T17's frozen dependency is T16 fishing/food; T18 also centers fishing. Level 1 needs meat and cooked goods, not fish. Separate the opening counter interface from the later fishing/road/fully staffed logistics extensions. The opening must work with the player alone; the *unlocked* survivor adds bulk service, not a mandatory new NPC. T23/T31 then verify owned-worker participation against the same interface. Otherwise T32 can wait on later content simply to certify a working opening counter. Preserve formal dependencies unless an owner approves a change; prove the staged integration explicitly.
2. **Give dynamic offers a shared early owner and rule.** T10 requires exact costs and idempotent transformations, T11 registers deterministic recipes, T13 tunes spend-blind challenge, and T33 owns balance but already depends on the approved T32 opening. Assign the *early eligibility contract* to T10/T12 with T17 as a consumer, then let T13/T33 supply approved curves and perform whole-game tuning. Do not add a reverse dependency on T33. Before a basket or quote can be active, prove attainable type, quantity, route, prerequisites, inputs and affordability. A producer plot can make an item attainable only if that plot can itself be afforded without the output. Never silently reprice a published payable quote or reroll an order on reload.
3. **Explicitly supersede contradictory loss rules.** The published CD01/CD02 rules say permanent sanctuary, free restoration, and no permanent casualty. Kaleb’s new direction requires pressure when the player and pets are too far, high-HP breakable fences and gates repaired through plots, destroyed equipment bought again at Level 1, and dead survivors/pets bought again. Survivors may proactively **hunt wildlife**. Confirm hostile priority separately: normally player/pets or an existing camp objective; an unprovoking survivor isn't the first target unless the player/pets are too far, and a survivor who strikes a hostile can draw retaliation. Being sheltered is not a separate permission to override the latest exception list. Define breach, interior protection, and safe fallback separately. The furnace penalty is still a design decision; a visible cold emergency and reachable Level 1 rebuild is the audit recommendation, not frozen scope.
4. **Define which camp is at risk across ten regions.** T44–T53 promise connected travel and preserved friends/cargo; they do not settle whether routine inter-region travel exposes every owned camp, which camp gets an active siege, how warnings reach the player, or whether a travel route can fail after departure. My recommendation: only explicitly active/exposed settlements can run a finite, warned siege during active play. Never infer ‘unattended’ from the render camera, a loading screen or real time spent offline. Preserve a solo return/recovery anchor; replay a departure, a return mid-siege, a changed route, and an app close during the same raid.
5. **Prove recovery from combined loss, not one subsystem at a time.** A failed saw cannot require planks only that saw creates; a lost counter cannot require cash obtainable only by that counter. Test losing the furnace, processor, and last available helper with no stock on hand. A normal $0 player must still gather what they need and rebuild. T14/T43 must preserve exactly-once sale, paid damage, casualty, repair, and replacement across restart, update and cloud conflict; no merged saves that resurrect a dead actor or grant both a refund and the goods. T37–T40 must exclude or safely pause temporarily unachievable event objectives, not consume inputs for an impossible task. T64 needs a separate decision for purchased entitlement vs. an in-world mortal actor; replacement is not automatically a second real-money charge.
6. **Fix acceptance contracts before collecting more screenshots.** The T54 opening audio text still specifies a continuous route; use multiple separate uncoached routes, with reproducible recordings for failures, and equivalent coverage muted. The whole-game plan says landscape-only; T55's older wording demands portrait and landscape, and Build 13's configuration permits portrait. Keep both supported landscape rotations and reconcile the fixture, not a silent waiver. T68/69's native internal 4K/60 gate remains distinct from screen refresh, desktop footage, and the optional 120-FPS objective. Certify the exact signed playable build on supported hardware, including a busy camp during a siege or transfer.

## Recommended order
- **Codex’s T03–T05 foundation review:** multi-route gate/camera and station review; capture accepted designs and interfaces without forcing siege into gate-art signoff.
- **First integrated change:** run on the Build 13 foundation. Shared typed stock, order/payment receipt, attainable quotes and save migration; meat-only opening; player-only and unlocked-survivor variants; belt uses same reservations. Review normal, blocked, interrupted and restored cases on separate routes.
- **Second integrated change:** legal targeting, hunting, a warned raid, structural breach, casualty/rebuy and a guaranteed emergency return/rebuild. Couple collision, feedback and persistence to the same committed outcome.
- **Expansion:** prove the opening under stress and at $0, validate each region’s new resource, demand, hazard and return obligations, then evaluate exact-build release. Keep the 9.9 reference-sensitive floor and stronger exact-10 visual gates. Nothing below activates a locked task.
