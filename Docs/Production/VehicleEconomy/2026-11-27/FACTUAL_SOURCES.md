# Crude oil and fuel — factual source boundary

Primary sources checked on 2026-11-27:

- [U.S. Energy Information Administration — Refining crude oil](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/oil-and-petroleum-products/refining-crude-oil.php): refineries convert crude oil into petroleum products, including transportation fuels such as diesel and gasoline.
- [U.S. Energy Information Administration — Where our oil comes from, in depth](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/oil-and-petroleum-products/where-our-oil-comes-from-in-depth.php): oil production uses wells, and collected crude is transported to refineries.
- [U.S. Energy Information Administration — The refining process](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/oil-and-petroleum-products/refining-crude-oil-the-refining-process.php): real refining includes separation, conversion and treatment. A simple game recipe abstracts those industrial processes.

These sources support the basic **outside oil well → crude oil → camp refinery → usable fuel** idea. They do not establish Havenline's recipes, costs, tank size, consumption or unlocks.

The selected **10 crude oil + 1 coal → 10 fuel** batch is an authored game abstraction using abstract inventory units. It is not a claim about real refinery yield, physical volume, engine compatibility or how to construct a refinery. The manual restoration/hand-pump work action is a gameplay choice for an existing reachable well site, providing a fuel-independent bootstrap. A later powered pumpjack is an optional productivity upgrade and must retain this manual recovery path.
