# Forward decisions and preserved history

The user's latest instruction explicitly introduces **low fuel consumption**, **iron bars and sheet metal**, **coal and oil use**, **expensive processed-resource construction**, and **vehicles for the later worlds**. It supersedes the earlier snow-truck package's “no fuel requirement introduced” scope decision. The old wording remains accurate as a dated record of that earlier request; it is no longer the forward fuel rule.

This narrow change does not waive unlimited mixed cargo, outside-camp inventory loss on vehicle destruction, garage-first purchase, the fence-line door, whole-cargo automatic warehouse deposit, existing U3 threat/recovery rules or the stronger project acceptance policies. Depletion alone is not destruction. Fuel already in the operating tank is separate from cargo and stays in the tank at garage unloading.

## Existing evidence

`SnowTruckGarage/2026-11-27/` freezes and reviews belong to their exact earlier source. They are preserved. New fuel source and tests require new review; earlier component approval does not approve the new code. New image proposals do not silently replace the existing snow truck or garage.

Existing paid/accepted purchase quotes retain their original bills. Owned vehicles are not charged again. Legacy vehicles migrate with no free newly invented fuel; the selected empty tank and reachable first-refill path must be communicated. New paid initial fuel must be allocated once from its actual quoted bill, not both spent and added free.

## Resource identity

The existing raw inventory key `metal` means ore in the current foundation. The selected compatibility design retains that stored key and presents it as **Iron ore** when the new catalogue is integrated. It does not create a second credited `iron_ore` balance. `iron_bars`, `sheet_metal` and `crude_oil` are distinct proposed new resources. Existing `coal` remains coal; existing `fuel` is reused for refined usable fuel, with legacy salvage semantics validated during migration. `steel` remains a separate processed material, not an alias for iron bars.

## Conflicting parallel planning text

The parallel planning branch contains a camp-wide blades-off recommendation and partial/unavailable warehouse unload wording. This package preserves the published truck package's scoped choice: blades default off in camp, deliberate eligible defense remains possible; docking and service require stopped blades; warehouse transfer remains all-or-hold. An unavailable warehouse retains every cargo item aboard until a valid whole transfer is possible. No conflicting source is silently treated as an owner-approved change.

## Proposed values

The 60-unit tank and initial tune of one fuel per 60 seconds of driving plus one per 120 seconds of blade use are authored test fixtures. With both operating continuously, a full tank lasts 40 active minutes; driving alone lasts 60. They establish exact accounting and an intentionally light fuel burden, not measured playtest balance or a fixed final price.
