## Decisions and practical limits

The current ore key remains `metal`; a display/version migration must not create a second balance or rescale legacy raw bundles. The new World1 chain is intentionally available before admitting the new truck quote. It does not require the missing truck or a trip to World3 to produce the first truck. New starter fuel is paid once from its bill and assigned to the operational tank; it is not also consumed as construction waste.

An empty tank denies powered use without deleting the vehicle or cargo. Garage return deposits every ordinary cargo item or holds the whole load if storage is unavailable, while operational tank fuel stays aboard. Outside destruction loses current cargo and tank fuel. Exact inside-camp fractional fuel estates remain distinct from ordinary salvage so they cannot deadlock replacement purchase.

World10 now proposes a portable serialized objective module. It follows ordinary exterior cargo loss. Any replacement must be paid, preserve the same objective serial and permit only one active instance and one installation. Exact replacement prices, source feasibility and that whole-world lifecycle remain unimplemented and unapproved.

The primary EIA sources support wells, transporting crude and refining it into transportation fuels. The selected recipe, coal input, units, hand-work action, tank and consumption figures are clearly authored game abstractions. No real refinery yield or construction claim is made.

The trusted adapter still owns truthful time, contacts, receipts, geometry and the complete-world epoch. A repeated successful fuel receipt is not permission to apply physical movement or harvesting again. Actual controls, safe exit, manufacturing, source renewal, route recovery, global custody/escrow, native meshes and motion, audio, playtest balance and physical devices require their own evidence.
