## Evidence hierarchy

Latest explicit owner decisions govern adaptations. Locked A/B pixels and motion anchor the core visual/interaction reference; selected GitHub designs supply declared Havenline assets; supplemental advertisements and storyboards broaden study; official documentation supplies context; engineering analogies inspire proposed implementation checks. These categories do not substitute for one another. Ad cuts do not prove real timers, recipes, throughput or balancing. A storyboard does not establish a complete motion cycle.

## Fresh primary-source context

[Century Games Snowbusters help](https://centurygames.helpshift.com/hc/en/64-whiteout-survival/section/1459-snowbusters/?l=en&p=ios&s=game-play) describes beasts/Snowmen, rescues, fire progression and stage resume. It also describes event fuel and reset rules. This helps identify that source context; Havenline retains its no-energy-wall and persistent-progression contracts. The text cannot identify the tiny YT13 figure visually.

[Wube #287](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-287) explains a junction timing repair using a short input holding segment. [Wube #442](https://factorio.com/blog/post/fff-442) explains clearer output-lane indication and connection/filter behavior. [Wube #443](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-443) describes separating stored inventory from physical unloading interfaces. These suggest Havenline tests for visible directions, rotation, short transfer buffers and compatible dispatch. They are engineering analogies, not Havenline visual references or authority for importing Factorio dimensions/rates/complexity.

The archived conveyor research also sampled five developer clips from those three Wube articles. That earlier sampling is retained with its own scope; it is not a new full playback or audio pass in this refresh.

## To earn the requested match

For each scenario, identify the actual candidate source, scene/state, source window, camera parameters, asset hashes and raw evidence. Compare normal gameplay first, then detail/multi-angle and full real-time/slow cycles. Trace physical goods and money through the full loop. Keep reference observation, owner adaptation and implementation acceptance as separate fields. Any unsupported quantity, unseen rear, inferred mechanic or unavailable source remains explicitly uncertain.
