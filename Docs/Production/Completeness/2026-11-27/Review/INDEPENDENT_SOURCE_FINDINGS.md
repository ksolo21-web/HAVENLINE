# Havenline completeness update — independent source findings

Separate critic audit of exact main `fd2e12c8734f49cda7eb3bbf97887406bd03a704`, 2026-11-27. This is preparatory evidence, not a final review or an implementation audit of every branch.

## Main finding

The original plan already owns onboarding/control, persistence/cloud, accessibility/device, LiveOps, diagnostics and release recovery. The important whole-game risk is that these broad requirements can remain disconnected from the new pet, survivor, resource and risk loops. The new plan should supply traceable cross-system closure cases and expose unresolved decisions. It must not describe every unrun acceptance case as a previously missing feature, or describe documentation as a finished game.

## Cross-system cases that would make the gap register useful

These are review expectations derived from the existing feature set, not newly implemented behavior or invented reference scenes.

1. **Fresh and returning player:** the player can understand where to go, why a material matters, what a helper/pet is doing, what a threat can cost and how to recover; help remains concise under actual camera, mute, reduced motion and smaller-screen layouts.
2. **Busy work shift with a legitimate threat:** player, survivors, customer, all relevant pet roles, machine output and cargo reservations compete without duplicate work, unwanted purchases, unreadable cues or repeated target thrash. Test one species at a time and the selected combined team; do not assume all seven must be active together.
3. **Interruption at every authority boundary:** reload after reserving, before debit, after contribution, at attack impact, after output creation, during cash transfer, after a recovery claim and at event grant. Exactly one committed result survives.
4. **Return/reconnect/account recovery:** specify offline advancement policy, recover the correct account's actual state, reconcile queued ordinary work safely, preserve earned/paid entitlements and never use played-time expiry as an unstated offline confiscation.
5. **Low-resource or partial-system failure:** core free progression and recovery work when a helper is unavailable, production is blocked, an event has ended, network authority is unavailable or analytics fails. A temporary unsupported premium/server action can be visibly pending/blocked without corrupting ordinary progress.
6. **Finished-world release route:** actual later-region resources, actor/environment compatibility, transitions, saves, accessibility options and quality modes remain consistent; final device and runtime evidence is exact-source-bound. A source audit does not prove this route was run.
