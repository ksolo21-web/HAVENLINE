## Selected forward design

Havenline is one persistent network of protected camps, working supply routes and rescued people. The player moves, gathers, carries, delivers, transforms, rescues, builds and defends; each region gives that loop a different practical problem. Older camps remain useful and their ownership persists. The game does not become ten separate tutorial resets or ten renamed currencies.

The root's current decisions are incorporated here:

- Survivors have **unlimited logical mixed cargo like the player**, with bounded representative visuals and bounded authorized job reservations. Ordinary job competence is baseline; suitable reachable equipment is a prerequisite, not an excuse for paid intelligence.
- Start with **three active field pets**, all seven permanently owned once acquired, and useful suitable local jobs for the rest. Selection is free at safe camp and cannot reset health, cooldowns, ownership or rewards. Every pet has a genuine species-specific attack; unsuitable regional combat may be inapplicable with a useful safe alternative, rather than inventing impossible anatomy or transport.
- Normal down/retreat causes **recoverable interruption and return**, with zero owned inventory, cash, plot/upgrade or named-companion loss. The home camp and last free recovery route stay viable. Exposed forward machines/defenses may enter visible repairable states.
- Background/close **pauses gameplay simulation** and resumes the exact valid state. There are no absence raids, fabricated offline earnings or routine idle drain. Offscreen areas during active play may continue authoritative, bounded jobs. Server event availability uses its own clock.
- Player weapons stay simple: contextual axe and an authored player spear, with ordinary material tiers, no action bar and no ammunition wallet.
- Level 100 concludes the journey, celebrates the earned world change and permits existing production, exploration and launch events to continue. Extra endgame activities remain post-launch. [S13]

These are new authored choices for this tightening. Their runtime behavior, balancing and animations still require evidence.

## Stakes, recovery and events

The player is avoiding failed field jobs, unsafe forward routes, interrupted rescue/service and repairable exposed equipment. Better preparation, useful workers/pets, clear route choice and relevant upgrades reduce those interruptions. The player is not avoiding a hidden bank wipe, permanent loss of a named companion, offline punishment or a paid revive screen.

The protected home retains a free source/tool/return path even after compound mistakes. Care and restoration after damage are free physical jobs with no material or cash charge. Initial construction, never-owned derelict reconstruction and elective improvements retain their disclosed project costs. Production pauses only for understandable missing input, blocked output, damage or unavailable route; it retains stock and resumes correctly. There is no compulsory equipment-decay tax, hunger meter, oxygen meter or paid energy wall. Every new hazard needs a visible warning, an attainable safe response, an exact consequence and a free recovery route; tuning remains measured work.

**The First Thaw:** clear a marked thaw-obstructed route; deliver existing wood/planks/food to restore a shelter/service point; defend a readable supply arrival; reveal the completed spring route and award disclosed existing goods/reward. Use the frozen-region assets and existing launch task families. A beginner-accessible variant must not depend on an unopened distant biome.

**Weekly/seasonal rotations:** compose approved service, rescue, restoration and defense components with attainable prerequisites and existing core goods. No temporary event wallet at launch, surprise confiscation, login-streak punishment or secretly changed base prices. Event expiry changes availability on server time; gameplay simulation remains paused while the app is closed. Earned rewards and purchased value survive valid reconciliation. Actual schedule, amounts and reward IDs still require authored records and T65 rehearsal. [S7][S8]

**Level 100:** finish the final protected connection, commit completion once, show the earned world/audio milestone, offer existing share behavior and return to the existing playable world. New mastery-contract families, new decoration systems, more regions and expanded Level-100 activities are explicitly outside the 1.0 critical path. [S13]

## Dependency-aware completion path

1. **M1: Select real content and value.** Adopt the 100 goals, canonical uses, Cash reinvestment, protected-progress failure policy, pause/resume semantics and Level-100 conclusion. These are usable implementation instructions, not future questions.
2. **M2: Close the opening loop on the authorized candidate.** Bind current receipts and migrations; implement useful material and cash sinks; integrate competent workers and all seven species' actual attacks with the selected team policy. Prove a real earn → carry → spend → visible benefit → failure → free recovery route.
3. **M3: Extend the same game through the nine further bands.** Implement each distinct route/production problem and exact cross-region custody; author the named launch event/rotation catalogue using approved components.
4. **M4: Measure and verify the whole journey.** Set final numbers from actual normal-player routes; run $0 progression, paid-value, event, persistence, security, accessibility, motion, audio, reference and device checks. Never substitute a 100-row plan or simulator output for the played game.

The JSON companion contains every authentic T01–T70 row with its existing dependency list, relevant owner/consumer gap IDs and explicit disposition. It records T71–T80 as undefined. This is a bounded progression/economy census; foundation visual work and other parallel workstreams are not silently declared complete.

### Acceptance boundary

The audit/authoring result can close selected design choices. It cannot close costs, balance, full-game implementation, rig/animation or performance without evidence. Final reference comparisons remain at least 9.9 per required dimension, with stronger exact 10 visual/style rules, complete mandatory evidence and independent review. Missing evidence stays blocked.
