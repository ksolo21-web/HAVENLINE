Local source checks must establish determinism, finite normals/UVs, closed outward component surfaces, negative controls, exact old bounds and unchanged authority. Model source construction is not visual approval.

Require actual Godot mobile-renderer family/closeup/perimeter/portal views at normal and native 3840x2160 scale-1 resolution. Compare matching reference pixels. Verify all six portals, terrain contact, opposite angles, no clipping and no changed opening behavior. Closed/open use-state inspection and motion remain required for final acceptance, alongside impact-appropriate regression and measured performance. Do not increase budgets to accept excess geometry.

Every applicable critic dimension must exceed 9.0 unrounded; required global style dimensions retain their stricter exact-10 policy. Complete coverage, medium/high confidence and zero unresolved defects are mandatory. Independent critic execution must be real. User visual signoff remains required. Historical 11H scores cannot approve the new asset family.
