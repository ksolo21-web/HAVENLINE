# Havenline post-launch roadmap

This roadmap is explicitly outside the release-critical T01-T70 production path.
None of these items may become a Havenline 1.0 blocker or leak into an active
task without a separately approved plan revision.

Potential post-launch work:

- more Level-100 activities;
- new event families;
- richer secrets;
- more companion cosmetics/interactions;
- controlled decoration zones;
- deeper survivor personality;
- expanded photo tools;
- asynchronous friends/Haven visits;
- community projects;
- optional future 2-4-player co-op;
- new regions/expansions;
- future level-cap increase only when meaningful new progression exists.

Explicitly not part of the Havenline 1.0 direction: unrestricted building,
mandatory guilds, guild warfare, free-text global chat, mandatory multiplayer,
energy systems, or button-heavy combat.
