Reviewer: `/root/independent_critic`. Scope: actual generated concept sheets, full-sheet and detail inspection. This is not an inspection of a native 3D model, animation or playable game.

## Result

The truck and garage are ready to present as authored concept proposals. No major static concept defect remains in the inspected views. Truck concept judgment: **9.7/10**. Garage concept judgment: **9.8/10**. These are interim concept scores; the project's stronger final 9.9/exact-10 reference and shipping gates remain open. User design selection also remains separate.

The garage shows front, rear/camp side, both sides, open parked use and roof-removed docking. Its outside door occupies the fence line; the open rear provides camp access. The right vent and left window remain coherent across views. R4 has one matching truck in each use view. The cutaway correctly shows the rear cab bulkhead and small rear window. The open-garage blades are fully behind the jamb, with visible floor clearance before the door plane.

## Explicit limits

Small fitting and cargo details vary between generated views; these images are not dimensionally exact modeling blueprints. A native model needs one explicit geometry source and actual multi-angle validation. Static illustrated clearance does not establish real collision or navigation clearance.

Driving, steering, blades in motion, target damage/harvesting, fence/door obstruction, docking and storage integration, audio and physical-device behavior have not been tested through these pictures. No game-integration or production approval is granted.
