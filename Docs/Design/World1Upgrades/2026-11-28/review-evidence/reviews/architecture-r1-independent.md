## Mandatory repairs

### AR-01 — Guardwalk observation platform

The first-stage observation structure still reads as the source’s cross-braced equipment pedestal placed above the walk. Its upper slab and socket have no usable human access. The lower bay is obstructed by the cross-braced framing and does not establish the promised occupiable observation platform. The stairs reach the main walkway; they do not prove access to this raised top.

Use a single occupiable observation floor flush with the guardwalk. Remove the inaccessible upper socket slab. Give the occupied floor a low outer guardrail and a clearly open entry on the walkway-facing side. Structural braces belong beneath that floor or on non-entry exterior faces. If a canopy remains, use clearly tall posts and leave the occupied bay open. Keep this exact arrangement across all three views.

### AR-02 — Fortified lookout entry

The roofed upper deck is closer to a usable observation area, but its walkway junction remains ambiguous: the railing, raised threshold and post overlap do not expose an unmistakable entry. A hidden opening cannot satisfy the access requirement. Exact standing clearance cannot be certified from this drawing.

Make the floor flush with the walkway, or show the entire shallow step and open landing. Remove the rail on the walkway-facing entry bay and show that gap in both the interior and high access views. Raise the roof supports enough to make standing room unmistakable relative to the guardrail. Keep the supply alcove below and outside the admission route.
