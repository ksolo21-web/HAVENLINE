## Revised mandatory review gates

1. Every proposed tier identifies the baseline limitation, the new useful operation or player choice, the visible mechanism and the reason it is worth buying.
2. Production art shows real input storage, working path and separate output handling. Additional heads/work cells must be visibly fed and able to discharge; duplicated machinery alone is insufficient.
3. A new processing lane is distinguished from larger logical stock capacity, faster cycles, a new recipe and automation. Promised functions remain authored proposals until built and tested; resource identities are conserved.
4. Expansion preserves playable walking, gate, service, manual delivery, worker approach and conveyor/truck clearances. Ports may change, but must be consistent across views and bound to the new stage.
5. Storage-house progression is mandatory. Receiving, typed reserves, dispatch, crew/truck handoff and structural protection must change meaningfully and visibly. Overhead views must show usable internal/external paths and distinct bays.
6. Fence and tower stages provide actual defense/attack differences. External strike zones, friendly movement, firing lanes, crew access, reload/ammunition storage and repair access must be understandable. Proposed crafted ammunition requires an attainable material chain; raw ore is not ammunition.
7. Survivor improvements cannot resell unlimited mixed-resource logical inventory, existing jobs or already authorized actions as new abilities. Gear and training must create a specific additional work, survival or coordination benefit.
8. All seven pets already have attacks and useful specialties. Pet upgrades retain species identity and improve a real species-specific capability. Gear must fit anatomy and movement; an owl cannot receive bulky ground-animal armor.
9. Show more than one eligible living pet/survivor simultaneously. Do not invent a new cap or imply that all seven species unlock in World 1. The earlier staged three-pet/two-escort expedition proposal and separate local workforce remain a source record, not a new owner approval.
10. Preserve ten worlds × fifty local levels, all authentic task identities and current evidence boundaries. Fifty world levels still do not prescribe fifty skins per object.
11. Review actual images with meaningful angles and use-state/interaction evidence. No score for rendered quality is awarded from a prompt. Static images cannot establish production rate, animation, combat, balanced value, runtime integration or device performance.
