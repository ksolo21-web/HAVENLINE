## Current scope

The owner now specifies 10 worlds with 50 levels each, and World 1 is the immediate art focus. World levels, equipment levels, appearance stages, state variants and separate resource identities remain different concepts. No new numeric equipment caps or unlock assignments are invented here.

## Open work

The complete World 1 appearance-level register remains open. Each actual appearance-changing upgrade still needs a level binding, retained base, explicit visual delta, cost/recipe relationship, actor permissions, footprint/ports and full native use-state proof. Art proposals can be generated now without pretending that those mechanics or numerical bindings are approved.

The existing numeric furnace labels and Atlas examples retain their original meaning. A/B/C labels do not become levels; cooked/raw resource forms do not become upgrades; open/closed/cutaway views do not become new building tiers; actor skill levels do not automatically authorize different bodies or outfits.

Exact per-family source paths, pinned URLs, local availability and hashes are in [WORLD1_UPGRADE_CENSUS.json](WORLD1_UPGRADE_CENSUS.json). The 15 generation prompts are in [UPGRADE_RENDER_BRIEFS.json](UPGRADE_RENDER_BRIEFS.json).

No baseline image is modified or overwritten. This inventory does not activate tasks, alter owners, approve runtime, or declare a complete playable World 1.
