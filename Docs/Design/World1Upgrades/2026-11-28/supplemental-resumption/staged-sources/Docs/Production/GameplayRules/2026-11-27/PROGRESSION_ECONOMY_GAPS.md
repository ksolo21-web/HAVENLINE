## Selected rules that apply throughout the game

- **Survivor cargo:** Player and actually unlocked living survivors carry unlimited logical mixed raw, processed and ordinary-cash quantities; physical stacks aggregate; useful jobs and purchase budgets remain bounded.
- **Baseline competence:** Every unlocked living survivor has baseline competence for every ordinary reachable job, including gathering, assigned hunting, hauling, counter stocking, all-cash pickup, deposit, authorized plot purchases and paid repair/rebuild. Equipment/reachability is real; upgrades improve throughput, never unlock basic intelligence.
- **Pet team:** Three active living field pets initially; all seven species remain permanently unlocked once acquired. Live instances can die and require ordinary-resource replacement. Earned levels/XP/skills/unlocks/paid entitlements persist; replacement creates a new instance without cloned estate cargo. Free safe-camp selection cannot revive, heal or reset cooldowns.
- **Pet attacks:** Every species has a real usable species-specific attack in applicable play. Regional combat may be inapplicable with an honest useful safe assignment.
- **Normal failure:** Player down preserves owned inventory/currency/account progress and returns at full current health with controls/camera to a currently valid safe recovery anchor. A destroyed camp under siege is not a valid anchor; retain the initial safe arrival fallback. Pets and survivors can die permanently as live instances, and equipment can be destroyed; ordinary paid replacement/reconstruction is required.
- **Background:** Gameplay simulation pauses; exact valid resume; no absence punishment or fabricated offline earnings. Active-play offscreen jobs conserve state.
- **Weapons:** Two simple contextual autoattack families, axe initially and authored player spear; no action bar or ammunition wallet.
- **Post100:** Celebration and continuation of existing loops and launch events; additional Level 100 activity families remain outside 1.0.
- **Currency:** Ordinary money / Cash and one primary premium_gems / Gems family; migrate any previously selected premium-wallet identifier without deleting or duplicating value; no launch event token.
- **Hunting:** Assigned survivor hunting and initiated attacks are allowed under U3. Retain hostile-wolf meat and selected Tundra Stag prey. Friendly companions are never hunt targets or meat sources; enemies can still attack them. Boar and other optional prey art remain reserve.
- **Repair policy:** Damaged owned structures use material/tier/missing-HP repair plots. Repair payments buy a fixed actual HP budget; new damage is not free. Destroyed fences/gates reconstruct their purchased tier. Destroyed equipment and furnace return at level1 after paid repurchase, and their upgrades must be earned again. Unconsumed escrow returns; already consumed repair work is not refunded while retaining healed HP.
- **Optional pet path:** Pet rescue/availability objectives are optional and accessible from the main route. Every required campaign outcome is completable with no pet deployed; player/worker defense remains valid. Owned deployed pets add real useful attacks and work, not mandatory competence gates.
- **Counter:** Bulk self-service for the full eligible processed catalogue. Player/unlocked living survivors stock requested products and collect/deposit all ordinary cash. Customers take a whole reserved basket and leave exact physical cash in a separate stack zone at one atomic contact commit. No cashier, register or spawned attendant role.
- **Quotes:** Authored progression band, actual reachable catalogue, target type/tier and stable seed/offer ordinal determine new types/quantities. Prove full raw/recipe/producer/bill/tool/route quantities before admission. Freeze accepted terms and partial contributions across progression, cancellation and reload; use explicit paid-generation rebinding after counter replacement, never a price reroll. If a bound revisioned world authority proves an unpaid basket unobtainable after counting its actual usable stock and own escrow, retire that identity without income, preserve all terms/history and admit only the next ordinal. A valid, fully reserved or settled basket cannot use this transition.
- **Furnace blackout:** Furnace0HP pauses new arrivals, automated production and ordinary non-recovery jobs in its camp. Preserve owned stock/cash in live custody or typed salvage; permit manual gathering, recovery hunting, salvage/cash pickup, combat, authorized purchases and repair/rebuild. Repurchase furnace level1 using a reachable raw-only bill; re-earn upgrades.
- **Targeting:** U3: a valid survivor attack attempt provokes that enemy. While the player OR a living deployed eligible defending pet is near, prefer valid defender/camp targets over unprovoked survivors. When BOTH are far, all characters and destructible camp equipment are eligible. Ranking is not collateral immunity or permanent camp sanctuary. Do not directly select an unprovoked survivor as a fallback while either valid defender remains nearby; actual contact may still cause collateral damage.

## Feasible dynamic quotes and bulk self-service

- **Processed sale catalogue:** meal, planks, blocks, cooked_fish, bread, steel
- **Catalogue condition:** Each product enters only when its actual source/recipe/producer, physical handling and customer depiction are authored. The native catalogue is a test fixture; no additional item is shipping merely because it is listed here.
- **Raw exclusion:** Raw wood/stone/meat/fish/wheat/ore/coal/slate, wrappers and premium currency are not implicitly eligible processed counter goods.
- **Feasibility:** Construct a full acquisition plan over unreserved owned stock, shared finite/renewable reachable inputs, permanent tools, current live producers, attainable plot/rebuild costs, prerequisite producers and recipe batches/output capacity. Fail cycles and unavailable quantities; never count this order payout as existing funding.
- **Price inputs:** Authored catalogue version, progression band, target type/tier and stable offer ordinal/seed. No current-wealth or paid-history price coefficient.
- **Lock:** One persistent obligation per actual target/tier/generation; bounded current customer/project slots; item/amount/reward/progression snapshot and contributions remain fixed through normal progress/save/cancel.
- **Interruption:** Unsettled basket escrow returns to counter or ruin salvage before evacuation/death/destruction. Settled customer goods remain customer-owned and never resale loot. Quotes retain terms; genuinely paid physical replacement can explicitly rebind the same customer obligation to the next generation.
- **Custody:** Logical totals are independent of stack mesh counts. Owned stock, carried goods, counter cash, storage, live escrow, persistent salvage, sold customer goods and legitimate spending form one conserved ledger.

- **Infeasible retirement:** An integration-bound revisioned world adapter and actual custody ledger must prove the whole unpaid basket unobtainable. Include usable living-player/unlocked-worker cargo, stock, storage, recoverable salvage and own escrow without double counting; exclude other customers and locked actors. Revalidate both owners before a zero-income retirement. Preserve immutable quote terms/ordinal and leftover goods; only the next offer ordinal opens. Ordinary cancel/reload cannot reroll valid orders; whole-reserved and settled baskets reject. Saved slots must match each counter's unique highest issued ordinal.

The native components use constructive quantity proofs, full-basket reservations, explicit sale commits, idempotent requests, strict saved-state validation and authenticated paid-generation replacement. They do not generate a finished100-level production catalogue. Actual plot/source/recipe IDs and animation contact events remain implementing-owner work.
