# Havenline — whole-game completeness and selected decisions

**Every pet attacks.** Guardian dog, gray wolf, fox, owl, lion, tiger and bear each need a useful, species-specific attack **and** a useful specialty. A scout, fetcher or warning effect cannot replace combat. This forward standard turns the known game-design gaps into selected behavior, task responsibilities and evidence requirements. It does not claim the game is finished or that an audit can discover every future defect.

## What the player is protecting

The player protects living companions, useful cargo, working defenses, machines, production and the camp's ability to trade. Enemies can kill pets and survivors, break high-HP fences and gates, and destroy placed equipment. Warnings, positioning, an actually present defense team and sound camp preparation prevent those setbacks. These are selected gameplay rules, not facts inferred from reference art.

The player retains owned cargo/progression on down and returns with working controls to a valid safe anchor. Companion death instead requires an ordinary earnable-resource/cash replacement purchase; earned companion progression and account entitlements remain, while cargo is claimed separately from one persistent estate. Damaged equipment uses material/severity-based repair plots. Fences/gates reconstruct at their purchased tier; other destroyed equipment must be rebought at level 1, then upgraded again. No mandatory real-money recovery, absence damage or hunger/stamina/oxygen maintenance meter is introduced.

The camp interior is physically defended, not invulnerable. While the player or an eligible living defending pet is near, enemies directly target reachable player/pet/camp objectives. Unprovoked survivors are not fallback targets. A survivor may hunt or initiate an authorized attack, provoking retaliation from that enemy. When both player and defending pets are far, all characters and destructible camp equipment can be targeted. Real collateral contact can still hurt a survivor. Visitors evacuate; their interrupted transactions conserve goods and payment.

Furnace destruction causes a local camp blackout: automated production and new customers pause, and ordinary worker jobs yield to recovery. Manual gathering, recovery jobs, combat, salvage, repairs and companion replacement remain possible. Rebuild the furnace at level 1 using a substantial reachable raw bill and re-earn its upgrades. A finite defeated siege retreats without victory rewards; new scheduled camp raids pause during rebuilding. Exact costs and timing remain measured-balance work. See the [full current recovery and threat rules](GAMEPLAY_RULES_STANDARD.md).

## Capable survivors and distinctive attacking pets

Survivors share the player's **unlimited logical mixed carrying policy**. A visible stack is an aggregated representation, never the actual capacity limit. Jobs still have bounded quantities, budgets and useful return conditions: a worker must not gather forever simply because inventory is unlimited. Every unlocked survivor can assist with hunting, harvesting, mixed hauling/storage, production/restocking, service/cash, authorized plot purchase and upgrades, construction/repair, defense, rescue/care, transport and coordination. All ordinary plot types include conveyors. Reachable equipment and a valid safe route are sensible prerequisites; paid basic intelligence and permanent job-family exclusions are not.

The selected expedition has up to **three pets and two survivor escorts**, with useful eligible camp/local assignments for the rest. The escort limit does not cap separately assigned local workers. When no eligible safe job exists, an honest ready/rest state is valid; useful AI must not become stuck idle while authorized work is available. These are authored starting limits, not performance claims. All seven remain ownable, useful and capable of attacking in suitable environments. Free switching at a valid safe camp must preserve injury, cargo, jobs and attack recovery; it cannot revive a dead instance or clone its estate. Quality settings cannot silently reduce the number of logical jobs or create a different economy on slower devices.

The [actor contract](GameplayRules/2026-11-27/ACTORS_COMBAT_GAPS.md) binds all seven to their existing exact GitHub sheets. Every species must prove real damage, a miss, interruption, recall, recovery, return to its specialty and use in later suitable progression. Mouth/talon cargo must be secured before an incompatible bite/strike. Friendly pets, hostile animals and hunt resources have separate identities.

Smart behavior means finishing useful jobs under changing conditions. It includes stable priorities, reservations, route recovery, shared engagement space, truthful intent and safe alternatives when a target is unreachable or a destination is full. Navigation avoidance is one mechanism; it cannot substitute for that job behavior. T07's approved human-context scope stays intact; species/T31 owners implement animal and job arbitration.

## A connected journey with worthwhile materials

The dated [progression and economy plan](GameplayRules/2026-11-27/PROGRESSION_ECONOMY_GAPS.md) supplies 100 authored outcomes within the existing ten-region cadence. Each level needs a useful visible payoff, and each region needs a distinct production, route, restoration or defense problem. The original T12 role IDs and resolved T10/T11 bindings are retained; those structural records alone were not 100 authored game outcomes.

Customers self-pick requested processed products from typed counter piles and leave physical cash in a separate pile. Player/unlocked survivors stock and collect in bulk; no attendant or register is created. Customer combinations and plot/upgrade resource types and quantities vary with progression, with complete acquisition proofs and stable accepted quotes.

Core resources continue to matter across regions. The selected chains give coal, ore/metal, slate, planks and blocks actual uses, and earned money has real reinvestment sinks. Each shipped identifier has an acquisition path, useful destination, tangible payoff and surplus/retirement policy. Containers and alternate art are not automatically new inventory items. The complete concept dispositions remain separate from the canonical economy.

There is one ordinary earned-money currency and one primary premium currency, **Gems** (`premium_gems`, subject to explicit migration from any already selected wallet ID). Till, pocket and house are custody locations for ordinary money. XP is progression; VIP is an entitlement. Launch events use existing resources/currencies and explicit rewards, without an extra token wallet. Exact quantities, prices, reward amounts and rates require reachable free-player simulations and playtests before producer integration; selecting a recipe does not prove it balanced.

Regions retain credible travel and animal behavior. Underwater work uses dry protected routes/habitats and work positions where equipment reaches salvage; the owl stays on suitable air routes or staging. Sky areas use connected platforms/bridges. Other difficult terrain has restored safe lanes and explicit return routes. Unsuitable pet combat in a location gets a useful safe assignment; it does not remove that species' real attack elsewhere or invent a maintenance meter.

Level 100 completes the restored network with an earned conclusion and celebration, then leaves the existing production, exploration and launch events playable. Additional endgame systems, decoration, co-op, new regions and a higher cap remain in the [post-launch roadmap](POST_LAUNCH_ROADMAP.md).

## The systems must agree at their boundaries

The [transaction and recovery matrix](GameplayRules/2026-11-27/TRANSACTIONS_AND_RECOVERY.json) covers twelve value-bearing boundaries: cargo, plot contributions, machine recipes, sales/cash, attack contact, defeat rewards, actor death/replacement, region travel, event rewards, purchases, structure damage/repair/rebuild and quote-feasibility changes. Each declares precommit state, the authoritative commit, postcommit state, interruption recovery and its clock.

Ordinary simulation pauses while the app is backgrounded or closed. A returning player resumes actual saved state, with no absence penalty or invented offline earnings. Offscreen jobs may advance during an active session using the same conserved ledger. Server time controls event availability and verified purchases/entitlements; changing the phone clock cannot create value. Launch event progress requires a verified connection and pauses clearly offline without consuming event inputs. A verified completion before expiry creates a durable earned claim that remains deliverable after expiry; interrupted delivery grants it once on reconnect. Until that receipt exists, the UI shows pending rather than earned. Ordinary core play and recovery remain usable offline; server-only value stays visibly pending or unavailable until verified.

First-use, returning-player and busy-camp tests must explain the next useful action, material purpose, threat consequence, helper intent and blocked action in context. Existing accessibility requirements apply to these actual states: non-color cues, readable text, touch targets, reduced motion, subtitles and persisted input preferences. A denied payment must not play a success animation or sound. Sound is reviewed through listening, including busy play and muted equivalents.

Every actor, tool, weapon, machine, vehicle and world-change interaction needs its applicable complete use states. Static renders and schema checks cannot prove contact, weight, full cycles, transitions or performance. Current reference dimensions remain at least **9.9 individually**, with all stronger **exact-10 visual gates** retained. Missing evidence blocks approval; no average or historical score can hide a failed area.
