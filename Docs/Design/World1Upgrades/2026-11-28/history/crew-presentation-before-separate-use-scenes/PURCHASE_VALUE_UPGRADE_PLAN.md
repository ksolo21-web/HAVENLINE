# World 1 upgrades worth purchasing

**27 authored value ladders, 54 substantial stage proposals, and an explicit disposition for all 99 retained family records.** The first art batch has four subjects and five sheets: sawmill, storage house, defenses, and two large survivor/pet crew sheets. This is a design proposal; no native behavior, final tuning, numeric unlock or owner art selection is asserted.

## What changed

The earlier reinforcement studies remain historical. New tiers may expand their purchased footprint and add useful working ports, provided routes, stock custody and clearances are defined. Accepted bases and the selected resource/conveyor unit scale stay intact. Storage, defenses, survivors and every pet species are now first-class value-design subjects.

## Existing abilities that are not sold again

- Survivors already carry unlimited logical mixed cargo and can perform eligible trained/equipped human tasks, including authorized hunting and attack.
- All seven pets already attack and have useful specialties. Their upgrades improve real training, protection, handling and coordination.
- The storage house already has typed bays, fuel separation and a central aisle. Its new value is receiving/dispatch separation, concurrent access, reserved staging, handling and finite protection.
- Free safe team switching and paid death replacement rules remain; upgrades do not create free revival.

## Simultaneous team policy

The latest owner request explicitly supports multiple pets and survivors together. The earlier staged CD04 policy proposed up to three expedition pets and two survivor escorts, separately from locally assigned workers and useful reserve-pet jobs. That prior policy is retained for review; it is not newly ratified or claimed integrated/performance-proven. No new total-population cap or paid slot is invented.
