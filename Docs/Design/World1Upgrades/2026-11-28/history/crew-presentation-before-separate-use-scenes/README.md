## Preserved history and limits

The earlier `UPGRADE_RENDER_BRIEFS` files and W1UP reinforcement studies are superseded as the active generation direction. Their evidence remains intact. `WORLD1_UPGRADE_CENSUS` and `UPGRADE_SOURCE_MANIFEST` preserve the original source census and exact baselines; their earlier no-actor-skins and storage-reuse-only dispositions are superseded by the purchase-value plan.

All 10 existing furnace forms and all three accepted storehouse stages remain retained. Approved conveyor and resource unit scale stays fixed. New purchased tiers may add footprint and working ports with explicit access and migration rules. A related source image is not proof of an unretrieved original asset, and a concept sheet does not establish a world unlock or native implementation.

The prior staged expedition policy of up to three pets and two survivor escorts remains recorded for review, separate from locally assigned workers. It is not a newly imposed total population cap. All seven pets already attack; survivor unlimited logical carrying and eligible basic tasks already belong to the baseline. Their upgrades must add measurable value beyond those abilities.

The five required priority sheets cover four subjects because crew stages receive one sheet each. A separately labeled unarmed architecture study is narrower evidence and cannot close the blocked armed-tower appearance requirement. Numeric prices, rates, capacities, protection and damage, species/world availability, complete appearance registration, full motion/audio and native gameplay proof remain explicit gates.
