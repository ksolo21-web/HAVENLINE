Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: Final square mobile game app icon artwork, 1024 by 1024, full-bleed opaque image.
Primary request: Create a striking, premium new app icon for HAVENLINE, a polished stylized 3D survival and camp-building game. Its identity is a warm safe hearth in a cold world. Make a single bold central sculpted hearth with a brilliant amber-orange flame, cool blue stone and soft white snow. This must be instantly legible when reduced to 48 pixels.
Subject: An original compact squat stone furnace/hearth built from a few chunky smooth rounded blue-slate stone blocks, with one wide arched fire opening on the front. A distinct three-tongued orange/golden flame fills that arch, bright warm core and deep orange edges, unmistakable at small size. A low round open chimney above contains a secondary warm glow. Thick sculpted snow rests on the upper outer rim and on the shoulders but never plugs the chimney or covers the flame. Warm light catches the inner arch and a small patch of snow directly below. A few broad, carefully sculpted blocks, not dozens of noisy pebbles. A confident front view with a slight elevated three-quarter angle showing depth.
Style/medium: Beautiful premium stylized 3D game key art, rounded and carefully sculpted, smooth tactile stone, soft sculpted snow, clean broad shapes, subtle high quality material detail. Saturated controlled color and crisp silhouette. Not photorealistic and not low-poly.
Scene/backdrop: Rich midnight navy at the outer edges, transitioning to restrained deep icy blue behind the furnace. A simple low snowbank grounds it. No other buildings, scenery, figures, tools or props. No busy texture. Background fills every pixel of the square.
Composition: Hero hearth centered. Entire important furnace silhouette and flame should fit within the central roughly 60 percent of the square, with generous dark blue breathing room all around for Android circle/squircle masking. The broader background light and snowbank may extend to the edges. Strong simple tonal separation between icy-blue stone, white snow, dark blue background and orange fire. Flame is the first thing the eye sees. Small amount of dimensional rim light; keep the hearth clearly readable without excessive bloom. No border.
Text: None. No letters, typography, logo wordmark, numbers, UI, watermark, rounded-corner frame, badge, glossy button border, split panels or mockup phone.
Quality: A fully resolved release-quality 2D icon illustration. Make the hearth feel welcoming, powerful and memorable rather than a gray product render.
