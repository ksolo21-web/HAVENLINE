# Havenline — proposed enemy roster 02

## Design status

These eight designs are **authored enemy proposals** created for the request for more and different enemies. Their roles describe intended design direction. They are not additional enemies recovered from the reference videos, source-defined runtime entries, or implemented combat systems.

The batch calls for an eight-angle turnaround and a six-panel closeup/static-action board for each subject: **eight concepts and 16 required sheets**. Image approval and GitHub delivery are recorded separately in the independent review and archive records. The existing approved artwork remains preserved.

## Proposed roles and silhouette differences

| Concept | Proposed role | Defining silhouette and identity locks |
|---|---|---|
| **Shield Raider** | Frontline shield defender | Broad, square human torso dominated by a large shield on the anatomical left forearm; short hammer in the right hand. Flat metal cap and cheek guards. The shield has a steel boss outside and straps inside. |
| **Harpoon Hunter** | Ranged tether attacker | Tall, lean female scout with a long upward harpoon and a small rope coil fixed at the left hip. The harpoon occupies the right hand; the long pole and tether give the figure a distinct outline. |
| **Powder Sapper** | Demolition/support attacker | Short, round human figure with padded goggles and a tall cylindrical backpack. Two broad shoulder straps and a sealed fictional charge canister establish the carried-equipment identity. |
| **Frost Stalker** | Fast feline ambusher | Lean snow-leopard-like torso, rounded ears and muzzle, smoky rosettes, large natural paws and one exceptionally long ringed tail. Four feline legs and a low stalking posture distinguish its body from the existing wolves and bears. |
| **Tusk Boar** | Low charging attacker | Broad, low russet-and-cream body, bristle ridge, pig snout and exactly two upward-curving lower-jaw tusks. Four short cloven-hoofed legs and one short curled tail keep it recognizably boar-like. |
| **Musk Ox Rampager** | Heavy charging/breaching beast | Massive shaggy shoulders and hanging fur skirt on four short cloven-hoofed legs. Two pale, broad forehead horn bosses continue into paired unbranched horns sweeping down, out and up. Its low quadrupedal mass separates it from antlered deer and upright beasts. |
| **Frostwing Raptor** | Flying dive attacker | Hooked beak, two powerful feathered wings, broad tail fan and two bird legs. The turnaround uses folded wings; separate static takeoff, glide and landing poses explore the proposed flying role. |
| **Armored Ram Sled** | Hostile siege-machine concept | Low, wide tracked chassis with one centered horizontal blunt ram, raised rear engine housing and one short rear exhaust stack. Two continuous tracks carry four road wheels each plus rounded front and rear end wheels. |

Each subject changes the roster through body shape, equipment or movement intent. These are design distinctions, not a new faction, tier system or claim that the proposed attacks already run in the game.

## Review and source boundaries

Review the actual full sheets and relevant enlargements for correct opposing profiles, a true rear view, continuous anatomy or mechanical parts, stable tusk/horn/wing/track counts, attached equipment and clear static support. Proposed action poses illustrate use states; they do not certify an animation cycle, rig, native mesh or runtime integration.

The earlier **YT13 red-crowd appearance remains unresolved**, and the YouTube full-stream and VidTao access limits remain in force. These eight creative additions do not identify that crowd, close inaccessible-source gaps, or retrospectively turn authored proposals into observed reference enemies.

Design authority: the roster requirements and independent proposal-design review for this batch. Image and delivery verdicts remain in their exact-hash review and archive records.
