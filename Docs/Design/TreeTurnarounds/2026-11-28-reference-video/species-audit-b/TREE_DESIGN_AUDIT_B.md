## What is consistent

The clearest intact trees show a pointed upper snow cap, overlapping middle and lower tiers, angular/V-cut skirt edges, pale blue snow with darker side/shadow faces, and a short exposed trunk/base. The small/front-edge examples use the same construction. Their broad lower tier can blend into shade or be hidden by a neighboring tree.

Apparent height, width and shade vary. The footage cannot establish whether those differences arise from scale settings, separate size meshes, camera effects or material/state variations. They do not, by themselves, demonstrate a second design family. Some trunk bases look darker than the usual red-brown; the cause is not conclusively assigned.
