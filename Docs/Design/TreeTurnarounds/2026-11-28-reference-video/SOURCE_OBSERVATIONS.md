## Visual specification

The inspected trees use three overlapping snow-crown tiers: a pointed small upper tier, a wider middle skirt and a broad lower skirt. Smooth cyan/icy-blue surfaces have pale upper highlights, deeper blue lower layers and narrow dark-blue undersides. Narrow triangular V-cuts form irregular angular lobes along each skirt. Slight apex curvature and silhouette irregularity matter. Trunks are short and appear red-brown or very dark brown in these different samples. No green needles or realistic bark/needle detail is visible in the selected crops. Soft blue-gray shadows sit on pale snow.

The additional 20-, 30-, 40- and 48-second frames show the same three-tier conifer visual family. They do not prove a second distinct family, an exact mesh/variant count, or a single shared mesh. Prior Havenline implementation has three models; that is not evidence that the source video contains exactly three source variants.

## Coverage limit

These inspected samples use an elevated oblique game view. They do not demonstrate a 360-degree orbit of one identified tree. Hidden rear geometry remains unverified. A turnaround can reconstruct those views consistently, but this evidence cannot certify literal 100 percent identity of unseen surfaces. The desired visible-view fidelity should still be preserved without substituting a generic Christmas tree.
