## Shared visual standard

Use the supplied polished stylized 3D appearance: mature readable body proportions, clean facial forms, natural individual fingers, detailed blue/orange winter clothing, cream shearling, leather gloves and boots, and soft studio lighting on a light neutral gray backdrop. Character 1 should match the supplied face, glasses, short curls, beard, garment shapes and proportions. His supplied design has no belt, pouch, backpack or harness.

Preserve the separate identities of Characters 2–4 while transferring this rendering style. Character 2 retains her straight side-swept bob, glasses, raised hood and blue bedroll. Character 3 retains her center-parted curls, burgundy inner layer, cream vest, open coat and tall framed pack. Character 4 retains her headband, hoop earrings, fingerless gloves, harness and brown bedroll. Their exact original image sources and hashes are recorded in the input provenance.

## Turnaround requirements

Each character has two four-view sheets: cardinal views at 0°, 90°, 180°, 270°, and diagonal views at 45°, 135°, 225°, 315°. Keep one consistent horizontal T-pose, full-body framing, true opposed profiles, head and torso aligned to the camera orientation, stable anatomical side placement of equipment, and coherent hands and feet. Normal foreshortening and occlusion are acceptable; detached, duplicated or incorrectly exposed parts are not.

The requested 2048×2048 size is an image-generation request, not an assertion of the tool’s delivered resolution. Record each actual native PNG size and retain the untouched generated bytes. Review readability at actual resolution.
