# Processing-machine designs and production roles

These six authored machine concepts extend the existing selected resource chain. They preserve the saved identifiers and do not create additional mandatory buildings for alternate recipes. Every output follows the selected resource identity: long dark iron bars; squat blue-silver stamped steel ingots; eight-lobed mechanical-parts gear; blue three-rib drive housing; amber-banded crude drum; tall orange X-panel fuel can.

## Machine roles

| Machine | Input | Output | Distinct operating action to implement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Iron smelter | Iron ore + coal | Iron bars | Ore enters the upper hopper; separate coal door closes; the firebox brightens; cast bars cool and advance to the side tray. |
| Metalworking bench | Iron bars | Sheet metal or mechanical parts | The hand crank turns paired rollers for sheets; the adjacent screw press clamps and forms parts. Only the selected recipe runs. |
| Steel forge | Iron bars + coal | Steel | Separate coal feed heats the arched chamber; the bellows wheel cycles; finished steel moves to its refractory cooling tray. |
| Assembly bench | Steel + parts + sheets; or steel + parts + planks | Drive assembly; or mobility assembly | The hoist lowers a workpiece into the jig; clamps close; the finished component transfers to the heavy output cradle. |
| Manual oil pump | Reachable outside-camp oil deposit | Crude oil | A worker turns the crank; linkage rocks the beam and reciprocates the rod; a sealed feed line fills the blue buffer; its separate valve dispenses crude to a drum. |
| Fuel refinery | Crude oil + coal | Fuel | Crude enters a sealed coupling; the separate coal heater cycles; the condenser feeds the orange-can filling point. |

These are animation requirements, not played or implemented animation. Each requires idle, start, full cycle, output-full pause, resume, damage, repair/reconstruction and save/reload interruption coverage in the game. Working motion must use actual contact, produce readable stock transfer, and conserve the current job.

## Manual access and conveyors

The displayed work shelves, cooling trays, bins, assembly cradle and liquid filling docks define intended service positions. Manual delivery and collection must remain available before automation. In-game solids routes attach only to the correct typed material port; coal stays separate from ore/bars. Oil and fuel require suitable container or sealed-fluid handoff. These concept sheets do not certify native socket dimensions, walkable clearances or a connected conveyor installation.

The pump is a reachable outside-camp source. The other industrial machines occupy later suitable production plots. Their addition does not enlarge or redesign the approved starting camp, move the central furnace or consume either required bidirectional gate.

## Dependencies and continuity

Existing sawmill, quarry, cooker, fish line and wheat processor remain the source references for their established chains. The six-check declared material graph confirms a fuel-free path to the first smelter and the first fuel batch. It does not establish final quantities, physical reachability or balance. Source IDs `metal` and `traction_kit` retain their existing meanings under the display names Iron ore and Mobility assembly.

Gems are currency. The relay component is a finite final-world project object; no ordinary factory recipe is introduced for it. The later restored relay worksite and its exact replacement recipe remain separately authored work.
