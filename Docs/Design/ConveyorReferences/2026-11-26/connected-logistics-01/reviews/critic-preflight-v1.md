## Design constraints established before generation

- Storage has front paired sliding doors/clear apron, a right **fuel-service hatch**, left frosted window and rear high round vent. None is an existing product port. Six perimeter zones and the center aisle must remain usable.
- The wooden customer table and blue processing counter are different assets. Customer-table register stays at its physical fixed end with controls facing staff; restock goes to a staff-side buffer beside the free stock end. Preserve four legs, lower shelf, serving surface, staff approach and customer queue. Cash stays separate.
- Cooker input crate is physical left; output tray physical right. Keep its central front control/door accessible. Clip-on transition trays are a new proposal.
- Historical catalog heights disagree between belts/stations and have no visual/runtime approval. New compatible transfer height and adjustment modules must be an explicit proposal, not assumed fit.
- Fish reference supports whole fish to cooked whole-fish-shaped output. Wheat to bread-like output is visible, but its intermediate recipe is unspecified. Raw-meat input and fat-capped rectangular output have source-view appearance evidence. No ratios, rates or fuel requirements are frozen.
- Saw/mill to planks is a proposed association. Do not serially connect every saw/mill or invent intermediate products. Furnace/hearth is a warmth/upgrade device; no smelting or food-processing output is established.
- Raw logs or raw meat may be legitimate sale goods when a counter's item catalogue permits them. Transport must preserve identity; selling raw ore does not turn it into ingots.

## Simplest storage upgrade to draw

Use two independent typed receive/dispatch cassettes: **utility on the left wall**, **clean finished food on the rear wall**. Each needs a distinct IN, OUT, bounded holding tray and short visible internal tray/lift ending directly in its designated perimeter bay. New low sleeves sit separately below the existing high window/rear vent. The front door, fuel hatch and center aisle stay clear. Declare the small added exterior service footprint.

For the first automatic upgrade, connect utility timber/material bays and prepared-food storage. Keep raw food, fuel, hides/fur, cargo and cash manually serviced unless their separate typed mechanisms are shown. This reduces crossed routes and avoids implying every shelf has automatic retrieval. Through-wall automation is a **new upgrade proposal**, with no inherited video approval.

## Acceptance checklist

1. Source-supported and proposed recipes/ports clearly distinguished.
2. Meaningful machine → machine, machine → storage and storage/production → counter connections present.
3. One-way arrows and transfer ownership; no duplication or immediate store-in/store-out loop.
4. Correct item compatibility and counter catalogue; raw/finished/fuel/mineral identities preserved.
5. Storage identity, new proposed ports and internal bay transfer visible.
6. Counter construction, register orientation and staff/customer/cash separation preserved.
7. Station front controls and side input/output access clear.
8. Believable widths/heights, transition plates, supports and uninterrupted carry surfaces.
9. Mixed or wrong items rejected safely before an incompatible lane; no silent disappearance.
10. Full counter/storage and blocked downstream cause stop-before-release/backpressure with bounded buffers.
11. Restock demand/reserve policy cannot recirculate items indefinitely.
12. Centered heater, two usable camp openings, player/worker aisles and customer approaches remain clear.
13. Straight/corner/merge/filter/stop-buffer/height-adapter family exists and matches Havenline style.
14. Actual plan, multiple module/interface views, closeups, normal and blocked states inspected.
15. Prompts, ordered inputs, originals, repairs, reviews and exact final GitHub readback preserved.
16. No unearned claim of working simulation, native mesh/collision, final balance or world-scale fit.
