## Independent release gates for this lane

- **External source/negative dependency:** exact imported sources, no legacy ring, shader wear mask, stock, enemy/pad aliases or real-save flags in active scene. Inspect actual SceneTree and file handles as well as source. Identical approved water vertices, indices, Y and all 10 anchors, three reserve masks and map edges. Reject a surviving flat floor, hidden legacy contacts, duplicate authority or parked sidecar known to progress.
- **Cell and surface parity:** left/right samples of each protected triangle diagonal at bank and apron; collider chosen from visible exact mesh. Reference accepted vertices/normals, then compare candidate including documented exported tolerance and the old T03 baseline separately. No terrain ownership between paid or unearned.
- **Paid and native path negatives:** actual normal controls for clean start, unavailable/failed purchase, receipt success, both gates both ways, settled/moving leaves occupied from either side, no-input drift, full and mixed carry, and no duplicate source/receipt on approved disposable reload. Invisible or painted path cropping is not proof; capture overlapping scenes before and after on the exact artifact. Do not exercise real saves or launch engine before Root opens that phase.
- **Bank/forest:** same-side boundary sprint and diagonal, reset into wet, an attempted reserve crossing should not open a bridge, both banks preserve accepted grade and width, exact 586 source-derived trees plus separately reviewed local decor and no arbitrary remesh. Real tree trunk seating and camera look on both normal and zoom. Release-critical reviewers independently inspect the exact candidate; passing offline design/launch does not pass the full level.
