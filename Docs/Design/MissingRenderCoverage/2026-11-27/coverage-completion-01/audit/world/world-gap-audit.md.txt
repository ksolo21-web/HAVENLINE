## Interpretation boundaries

- Static concept/reference art only; no native mesh, rig, motion, playable region or runtime approval.
- Preserve original69-slot history, prior selected/rejected assets and current three-tier storehouse.
- Atlas upgrade examples and future regions are explicit authored references, not implemented upgrade tables or an exhaustive future game catalogue.
- No inferred recipes, crop lists, species drops, ammunition, powers, costs, capacity or exact world-unit dimensions.
- Latest matching direct user design remains the identity authority; source-hidden rear/interior forms must be labelled interpreted.
- Do not claim an existing family is absent merely because a board uses another visual variant. Covered-family rows name their exact coverage and limits.

The lower atlas cast/animal/raider census is separately owned by the independent cast auditor. Source context labels and event icons are listed in the JSON as non-object categories, rather than converted into invented physical asset counts.
