## Scope rules

- Meat references do not count as live wildlife anatomy. Companion harnesses and packs do not prove unobstructed wild-animal forms.
- Same or similar names do not prove matching designs: the two Shield Raider designs have visibly different faces, clothing and shield shape.
- Branching elk antlers do not supply palmate moose antlers. Long-tail round-ear snow leopard art does not supply short-tail tufted lynx art.
- Existing Hostile Wolf and Polar Bear provide the two appearance-level coverage mappings. Their original names/source provenance remain unchanged; this is not a canon alias or a claim that their earlier prompts used this Atlas.
- All source cells are small single-pose concept illustrations. Unseen reverse anatomy/equipment is authored interpretation.
- New humanoid reference renders should carry the user’s five-separated-digit standard; held-prop poses can occlude fingers naturally, with dedicated open-hand reference where needed.
- Full Atlas PNG, native source cell crops, full selected sheets inspected for relevant near matches, all20approved enemy front pixels and exact hashes are linked in `atlas-cast-census-v2.json`.
