## Scoped verdict

**9/10 for transaction legibility in this field route.** The green pad fills in installments, the last wood produces a visible hearth, the court changes, each typed stock appears on its own plot and an earned purchase increases the orbit from one to three. On the updated landscape and portrait routes the actor can briefly cover a floor icon. The contextual HUD independently resolves its type and quantity. Both aspect ratios show “Build hearth · 2 wood remaining”, “Meat storage · 2” and “Upgrade · 1 stone, 1 meat remaining” at the corresponding recorded states. At 44.70 s in each orientation the first upgrade debit showed its outstanding cost of one stone and one meat. Small red parcels are still harder to resolve from a distance than the HUD figure. This is an independent practical score for this specific sequence.

A second independent reviewer scored each of six sampled visual checks at 9/10, including the previously failed mining visibility and physical stock counts. [Second field-camera review](../../../QA/Field-Loop-2026-11-26/References/Second-field-review.md)
