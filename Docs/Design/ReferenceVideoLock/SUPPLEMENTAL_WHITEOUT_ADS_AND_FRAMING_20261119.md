## 2.5D is the presentation, not an engine rewrite

Keep Godot's 3D meshes, rigs and world coordinates. Use a close, stable, elevated oblique view and mostly ground-plane player interaction. Havenline already uses orthographic projection; changing projection alone will not fix the composition. The reference footage looks orthographic but does not reveal its exact engine or camera settings. See [Godot Camera3D](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/4.7/classes/class_camera3d.html).
