## Builder extraction checklist

Use the pack to strengthen these already-required Havenline qualities:

- **Physical resource continuity:** show source → pickup → carried stack → machine/station → output → stock/counter → spend. Avoid invisible inventory-only transitions.
- **Readable upgrade causality:** the player reaches/spends at an in-world pad, the station visibly changes, throughput/defense visibly changes, and the new benefit can be seen immediately.
- **Dense but readable scenes:** many trees, workers, customers, machines, resource piles and threats can coexist without collapsing into noise.
- **Warm/cold contrast:** snow/ice remains clean and bright; active work zones, furnaces and fires create localized warm focal areas.
- **Threat with purpose:** bears/enemy waves test something the player just built or supplied; defense is not disconnected spectacle.
- **Oblique mobile composition:** maintain readable character/item scale and short travel paths; do not shrink the whole world into a distant strategy-board view.
- **Stylized finish:** clean sculpted forms, strong silhouettes, saturated blue/orange/yellow/green gameplay accents, coherent soft/contact shadows, no debug/default materials.
- **Havenline identity:** use Havenline characters, companions, naming, UI and art decisions. External references are quality/interaction cues only.
