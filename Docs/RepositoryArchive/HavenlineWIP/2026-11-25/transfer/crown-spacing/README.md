# Isolated crown-log spacing study

**Outcome:** the 35 mm point-only schedule cannot be used as a physical layout for aligned, source-size crown logs. A separated four-item test pattern can be dimensioned from exact geometry, but the current carry display and diagnostic counter spot are not two-row anchors or a bound receiver. No art exported or edited and no runtime, renderer, gameplay capacity or approval claim.

Read-only decode of the exact untouched glTF crown: 1,087+213 triangles, original X length / Y height / Z depth = 283.673 × 102.560 × 103.834 mm. Applying the fixture's identical 35 mm point spacing to translated, same-orientation copies produces **1,593 / 746 / 789 strict surface-crossing pairs** along X/Y/Z, respectively. These are mesh-triangle pairs, not numbers of items or broader overlaps. The strongest strictly positive intersection segment in the actual fixture axes was 56.755 mm in X (triangles 733/732) and 41.842 mm in Y (817/840). AABB pruning followed by signed plane straddle in both directions and strictly positive mutual line interval; coplanar/touching/endpoint-on-plane and near-degenerate cases are excluded, so the positive counts are lower bounds, not penetration depths.

Constructive same-orientation axis-separated spacing with a chosen 1 mm margin: **284.673 X / 103.560 Y / 104.834 Z mm**. This is safe bounded separation, not a measured minimum. Along one Z row, 26 logs need at least 2.6997 m of depth *if* all projected intervals must be disjoint; the explicit 1 mm-gap construction takes 2.7247 m. Two such rows along Y need an envelope 0.2837 × 0.2061 × 2.7247 m. A tighter four-piece fixture would be a 2 × 2 over Y/Z with the same orientation, occupying an envelope 0.2837 × 0.2061 × 0.2087 m. A larger 4×13 fixture reduces depth to 1.3618 m by using 0.4132 m height. None is a minimum over nesting, rotated orientations, tapered profiles, or another approved visualization.
