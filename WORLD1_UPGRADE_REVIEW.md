# Havenline — World 1 upgrade review

## What the first reviewed images cover

| Family | Visible change | Proposed working value |
|---|---|---|
| Storage house | Receiving wing and separate dispatch area; then a larger protected hall with a manual loading hoist | Concurrent handling, reserved order staging and useful loading work with one conserved stock ledger |
| Sawmill | Buffered feed cell; then two independent cutting cells | More simultaneous processing, real input/output buffers and reachable per-cell controls |
| Fence and lookout | Stronger boundary, usable guardwalk, protected lookout | Better defensive work positions and resupply access; armed-tower illustration remains blocked |
| Trained crew | Practical work protection and species-specific fixtures | Supply and operation together, with distinct pet guarding/scouting positions |
| Veteran crew | More developed mobile protection and working gear | Repair, guarding and warning together, with attack/protection gains specified for later measurement |

The seven files passed 83 mandatory static checks across the two independent art reviewers. They remain authored proposals awaiting owner selection. Static images do not establish gameplay integration, measured upgrade benefits, motion, audio or a complete set of World 1 upgrades. The armed-tower visual and remaining family/level registrations are explicitly open.
