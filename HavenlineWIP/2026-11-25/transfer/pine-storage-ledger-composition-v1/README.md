No production patch, stock tooling, or authority for any receiver, slot, site, material policy, ownership rule, storage cap, withdrawal, payment, price, or input. The one blank sink is an in-memory fixture object. This is not Godot, a true disk durability test, or a format release.

## Why a direct deposit is incompatible today
Exact detached v2 pine patch e88468b7… and native author seal 2939ea7d… are verified in full; the earlier typed transfer coordinator c901b967… and its unchanged staged planner are also verified. They establish (1) original same-pine purchase/start/settlement authority and v1→v2 opt-in save behavior, and (2) a typed move committed before presentation, not a save or a world source. Active pine v2 is deliberately strict: after receipt it checks stock.wood == pine_occurrences in both the live harvest gate and the restore arithmetic. With two legal paid cuts and a naïve deposit of one, carrying becomes 1 but historic occurrences stay 2. The active v2 refuses that decoupled state; a v2 bypass is not proposed.

## Bounded candidate contract
Keep exact starter IDs and the original immutable 3-wood hearth receipt. Keep separate positive monotonic harvest and deposit high-water IDs. Typed owned wood has two books: carried and stored; lifetime conservation is starter IDs used + accepted harvested = carried + stored + original scoped debit, and unpaid states cannot have any renewed/stored wood. A harvest adds one owned wood and advances only the harvest ID; a deposit transfers trusted positive units of that same typed row and advances only the deposit ID. The pending opaque trusted object is process-bound and not imported from a bare caller-supplied sequence, receipt or ledger object. The pure trusted-harness issue function is not player ingress or evidence about actual receiver authorization. Receipt is checked as exact independent history, not a quantity that the transfer can regenerate or change.

Proposed **test-only payload v3** reads valid original v1 and v2 payloads/envelopes without changing them. Old v2 count maps to harvest count, all old wood maps to carried, and stored/deposit counter are zero. Even unsorted envelope payload text checks against its exact string hash; legacy data types and receipt/ID conservation tested. New writes are v3 only in this test fixture (no downgrade claim). Single in-memory atomic promotion emits only coherent state, denies stale producer/sink, wrong source/material/receipt, untrusted, malformed, skipped and duplicate IDs, and compares prior durable state before promoting. Before-promotion interruption keeps prior bytes/stock; after-promotion interruption cold-loads next stock/ID even if old process never published; stale transient objects are not restored. Checksum and schema do not authenticate attacker-writable storage or claim multi-writer filesystem fencing, fsync or an OS-powered kill test.
