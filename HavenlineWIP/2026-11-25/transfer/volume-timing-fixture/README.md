Distinguish an event's scheduled due from the actual frame sample that observed it; a coarse 1,000ms skip delivers ordered signals then and is explicitly not a timing pass. The duration is a proposal guided by the observed reference, not derived exact source timing or adopted game policy.

Checks include 30/60/120Hz schedule sampling and jitter; exact per-ticket/ordinal arrived signals, reached transform before signal, a single completion, untouched other row/ledger, active and post-complete duplicates, negative wrong material/amount/receiver/untyped receipt/uncommitted/stale-watermark/stale-clock paths, and reentrant epoch replacement while dispatching.
