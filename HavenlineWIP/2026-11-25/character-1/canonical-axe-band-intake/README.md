Compare exact source `HavenlineGodot/assets/harvesting_v1/axe.glb` with the provisional PR #108 `HavenlineLevel1/assets/tools/axe.glb` (head 48c6c53a0dfda117ef12c68b32cfe2b3732d5239). They are byte-identical: 21,912 bytes, SHA256 79a3c9e4d31c609f0dc552b8810ab4061256def22e2eb9e4cefc3c1a33f3677e, Git blob 9d7ab5e3cbb42f64b3ce0451e6e5171954dc172e. The oversized appearance candidate uses an additional uniform scale in the kit at runtime (0.95/0.70) and is not a different GLB. Substituting the original file alone changes nothing. The measurement describes source scale 1 as a diagnostic; no game tool transform was changed and no source-scale pose was evaluated for clearance or approved.

At a 1 µm coordinate weld, 8 exact untransformed components account for all 512 triangles. One 55-mm-radius wood core runs Y −430 to +270 mm, with the distal +Y portion under head hardware. A conservative head-free envelope (Y −440 to +5 mm open, 445 mm axial span) includes overlapping sleeves/covers, not a uniform 445 mm strapless surface. Nominal exposed component-radius bounds are 69 mm on (−440,−310), 73 mm on (−310,−210), 55 mm on (−210,−150), 71 mm on (−150,−50), and 55 mm on (−50,+5). Treat sleeve edges and cap planes as boundaries rather than free hand support. The longest gross section without a change of selected outer component is about 130 mm; its radius bound is not exact ray distance every azimuth. None of these axial intervals can be called safe without actual glove, thumb, cuff, arm and body clearance.

The immutable axe manifest bands at source scale 1 are 260 mm apart. Tested native corrected-hand spans on the provisional rig were 218.70–296.42 mm chopping and 329.74 mm carrying. The source-scale pair would miss by up to 41.30 mm in chop separation (20.65 mm per hand if symmetrically centered) and a constant 69.74 mm in carry separation (34.87 mm each). The 445 mm pure axial envelope is long enough for both point centers over these samples; considering equal hand footprints centered at those points and ignoring all surface constraints, the maximum allowable full projected width with no end allowances would be at most 148.58 mm per hand at the widest chop pose and 115.26 mm at the carry pose. These are optimistic necessary bounds, not measured glove spans or sufficient placement.

Assignment safeguard: the exact provisional sort put R lower and L upper at every chop observation, but L lower and R upper at every carry observation. The source geometry itself does not select the player’s anatomical dominant hand. As a non-authoring test, preserve the measured chop's R-lower/L-upper identity through a continuous carry-to-chop path and reject a deliberately same-midpoint role swap. If staging cannot preserve such ordering while honoring actor anatomy, require an explicitly designed release/regrip transition. This does not authorize grip keys, a different weapon, resizing or pose adoption. Mine data are retained only as a spacing comparison, never as authorization to use the axe for mining.
