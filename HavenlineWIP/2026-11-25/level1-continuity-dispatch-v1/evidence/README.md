# Havenline Level 1: queue, handoff and bottleneck playbook

The active bot pool is bounded: Alfred coordinates up to six simultaneous workers in this room. A role on the organization chart is not proof of a live worker. The existing 24-hour hourly check and completion notifications are independent of any particular worker; they do not guarantee uninterrupted execution or override engine, disk, connected computer or approval limits.

## Assignment and dispatch
1. At each result or scheduled check, read actual statuses. Record the worker, job, current owner, source revision, test/evidence, pending dependency and next check. Use running, ready, awaiting a prerequisite and completed literally; don't mark a queued or waiting bot as active.
2. Give each finished worker the highest-priority distinct ready job. Multiple producers may own separate code/assets/fixtures, but only one author per output. Review, repair and integration remain separate: the independent critic tests the exact submitted version; an assigned repair owner fixes a reject and returns it to the critic, while builders continue ready work. No downstream claim before its matched evidence.
3. Bottleneck repair follows the missing resource. Add or repurpose a specialist if a real skill or review lane is missing. If disk, native access, exact input, owner authority or a single runner is missing, name that constraint and address it. Do not duplicate the same job to fill slots. Let other ready lanes continue while the bottleneck owner works.
4. Reserve the sole native/render lock for an admitted task with exact input, independent expected controls, explicit wall-time/space caps and output. Near-term order: finish active normal-camera recording and recover only its creator's proven regenerable isolated caches if released; then admit the V5 corner native witness or nearer verified runner if measured space and exact custody allow. Larger wood later needs its own reference-based A/B. Counter native witness needs its distinct 350 MiB reserve and is independent from interaction/receipt authority. Unadmitted tasks are held with a real next condition and are never called installed.
5. Continue isolated Level 1 only. Archive safe reproducible source/test artifacts to the replacement branch; retain production and independent critic gates. Do not publish drafts/ad recordings/private buffers, change default/production, touch user saves or promise a launch. Hourly reporting continues day/night and sooner for a meaningful result or decision, without duplicating a recent checkpoint.
