## Why not playable acceptance?

Actual sideways idle stances at the original, still-reachable markers cross the full donor (wood/dark apron inboard and blue apron outboard), although neither crosses the worktop in that sample. Existing marker reach is not authored interaction or physical visual clearance with all headings. The 3.0 m guard can also be slid around by a deliberately angled real input at the outer ends into the old bridge. The v3 output reports actual outer-end and retained seam mesh tests, not an inference from the guard surface. A failed surface test means HOLD, not a rendered clip or approval. A future shape/approach change must honor all visual, fence, gate and stance authorizations and be checked from real route directions; consider explicitly authored safe approach and facing/stance, not automatic marker movement, yaw overrides, or unsafe blanket collider growth. No images captured; original 16-image game-camera gate stands. Static posed base triangles are not moving interpolation, camera LOD, visibility/readability, or device presentation evidence.
