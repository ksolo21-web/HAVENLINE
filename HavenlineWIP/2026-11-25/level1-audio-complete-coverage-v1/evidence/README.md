The requirement is coverage of **each meaningful scenario**, including negative outcomes and deliberate silence. It is not one sound per button, frame or item when that would misreport outcomes or make the mix tiring. This is a 47-scenario assignment/checklist, not 47 adjustments installed. ## Read the matrix

- `coverage.tsv`: ID; scenario group; source authority; implementation state; layer; priority; valid trigger; where it should stay silent; response direction; actual device witness.
- `sources.tsv`: provenance and limits. Paths listed as BASE/... resolve inside the pinned Level 1 project. L1 is owner-confirmed scope, **not** permission to fabricate actor/transaction systems.
- State terms: **VERIFIED-INTENT+SKETCH** means an existing detached post-commit contract passed native proof and an offline sketch exists, not audio played by the game. **SKETCH** and **OFFLINE+INTENT** have narrowly labeled technical evidence. **L1-UNBOUND** needs a confirmed cue or trigger/audio work. **OUT-OF-SCOPE** is intentional exclusion unless the owner approves a narrower current Level 1 lane. None is marked installed.
- Priority: **P1** validated outcome/state or important feedback must not be drowned out; **P2** contextual effects can be grouped or capped; **P3** atmosphere, decorative foley or deliberately optional/silent. If P1 overlaps another P1, use short mixing/queuing only if it preserves meaning; do not silently move an effect out of sync with its visible outcome.
- `python3 verify_coverage.py` checks structure, source references, state vocabulary and authority holds; it cannot replay the game, approve assets, or score phone listening.
