# Fresh owner-reference fence 33036 geometry (one detached panel)

This is a newly authored candidate from Kaleb’s actual 1254×1254 fence turnaround 33036 (SHA 067b1e9f387882314094196dce1938f713875805f50902857499df6c376261e3), not the supplied render or visually approved in-game art. I inspected all four views of the sheet. This panel does not reuse or mirror the old 18595 X-wrap material or shape. It is not integrated, and the older V3/V4 art is not the design control.

Geometry: eight close thick amber log bodies, eight individually rounded broad white caps, two actual continuous front round rails (center Y .191 and .883 m, ~13% and 60% of overall height), and exactly two ivory woven loops total. Each loop is a continuous zigzag on the exterior rail, carries around both end profiles, and comes back on an almost-level rear run against the logs. Actual centered front rope path varies 84 mm vertically while the corresponding rear varies only 8 mm. No separate mirrored X on the back. End rings taper where they round the crossbar edge; stock-aspect maximum path turn 54.80 degrees. Four tint/roughness-only local materials, no source sheet maps, textures, shaders, edits to global lighting or claimed material appearance.

Exact signed render-time depth under the proven 1.95 depth scale and .00975m outward recenter is inside the prior visual corridor entirely: 284.48 mm profile versus 294.84 mm prior visual limit and .30m existing collision thickness, retaining 6.86 mm outward / 3.49 mm inward protected slack. Logs have measured 3.57–12.03 mm authored X separation (about 2.4–8.0 mm at the southwest scaling), not touching or overlapping. Rails are actually filled 3D solids with round cut ends. The requested rail depth fits without moving an anchor or widening any collider. Artwork metrics called out in the sheet, especially side x extent, are qualitative; the side drawings are gently oblique so direct image-width conversion to real mesh thickness would be unjustified.

All 20 closed shells on the serialized mesh have positive oriented volume and reciprocal edges; actual strict triangle contact at every own snow/wood cap, both front rope/rail levels near all eight logs, and rear rope/own log at both levels. No rope self-cross, contact with a different level/opposite rail, or crossing against any snow cap. Adjacent logs/caps do not geometrically penetrate. Does not imply static appearance at stock wall joins with world ground, exact post clipping at gate edge, or independent native art quality. Existing physical stock anchors/routes and gate interfaces are only measured, NEVER written.

Front local +Z, rear −Z, right profile +X, left −X. Stock layout mounting proposal with same row centerline/run offsets, existing width/depth scales and unchanged physics: South 0° (+Z exterior), East +90° (+X exterior), North 180° (−Z exterior), West −90° (−X exterior), retaining .00975 m art recenter along new outward normal. The old software art centre can sign-reverse its tiny visual offset under an outward orientation flip; this stays in the same physical wall corridor and is not a new stock gameplay anchor. At authored southwest scale and normal 1280×720 fixed-camera 720/13 px/m, analytic projection ~97.46×97.20 px; separate axis profiles ~15.76 px deep. Analytic FRONT rail coverage 1,004 px², each SIDE ~52, REAR 36 through narrow joins, confirming true front/rear geometry distinction and real side ends. This is a depth-only calculation from actual OBJ, not raster lighting, read grade or the owner's reference render pixel values.
