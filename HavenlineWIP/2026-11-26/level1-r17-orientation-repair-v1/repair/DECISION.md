# R17 fence dark strips: source orientation repair candidate (detached)

Exact R17 staging donors, merged surface tags, straight stock shader, original Level 1 daylight and one captured normal camera paid frame were inspected without running a renderer. The approved 18595 reference shows timber with curved tan crossed lashes, softly shaded grain and rounded tapered snow. In the actual capture the bare repetitious strips and faceted caps replace much of that visual read. Root cause diagnosed here is narrower: every closed donor picket/post/cap-body/accent solid is inside-out, and every open snowy peak face points inward/down. This is not simply dark albedo.

At source OBJ orientation (counterclockwise outward), all 26 closed prisms have accurate opposite edge incidence but negative signed volume; all 28 raw face normals in each point toward the interior. Each exported prism mixes 16 genuinely inward explicit side normals and 12 already-outward cap normals. All 11 open eight-triangle roof fans point down/inward as well. Example panel first face is on z=-.07, yet its raw cross and stored corner normals point +Z. The correct lower-face winding has cross -Z there. Godot's public OBJ importer source swaps the first two corners to convert to its clockwise front-face convention, without changing explicit normal vectors: that expected conversion cannot correct the supplied physical reversal. See https://github.com/godotengine/godot/blob/master/editor/import/3d/resource_importer_obj.cpp and https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_surfacetool.html. The original R17 world-X and world-Z bases have positive determinant; neither orientation nor the 1.95 visual depth scale rescues an inward OBJ.

One candidate changes **only face winding and inward explicit normals** in both detached donors. 640 panel +176 post triangle sets, 1,920 +528 source vertex lines, all actual labels/material tags, all source vertices, scaling, batch instances, seams, gates, stock shader, input, tracks, colliders, camera and lighting authority remain unchanged. 1,200 panel and 312 post inward explicit normals flip, while 720/216 already-correct normals stay exact. Every actual oriented corner normal has dot >=.99999997 with the physically outward face. No new prism, rope, material, highlights, root/offset or gameplay change. Do not combine with a fill tint for the first independent A/B.

Other limitations remain. The R17 shell is not the exact 18595 banded anatomy: the panel donor's only tags are timber (280 tris) and snow (360), with no rope/texture input; repeated octagonal prisms, closed cap bodies and open roof fans retain their exact existing geometry. The post has timber/snow/orange. Absent local warm shading/rope geometry and cap profile are independent conditions, not fixed by this patch. Runtime has one shadowed directional sun and no obvious environment fill in the pinned scene files; some corrected faces will still be legitimately shaded. The unchanged shader's only built-in nonzero material emission is the orange accent and its tiny multiplicative albedo variation cannot by itself turn positive albedo into black. The exact original PNG includes (0,0,0) in selected wall regions with no exact zeros in the flat-snow control, but not every pixel in the regions is an isolated fence surface; these are baseline diagnostics, not a predicted numerical improvement.

If the opaque side panels and peaks do not genuinely resume expected exterior lighting, investigate OBJ internal imported attributes without tuning palette; if an authentic shaded side still crushes black, measure that separately before proposing any material-local fill.
